# Proposed RXD protocols

Generated from the website protocol registry. Regenerate with scripts/generate-protocol-reference.mjs after editorial revisions. These are internal proposals, not validated standards, NIST methods or ASTM identifiers. The actual IDs/names below preserve the repository vocabulary.

All protocols currently require human review. Do not score real robots until scoring anchors and protocol requirements are approved. Revisions create new versions; retain old versions for historical references.

## Assessment modes

- Documentary research identifies advertised signals, controls, intended workflow and documented recovery paths. It does not establish observed behavior or user comprehension.
- Remote observation can establish visible/audible behavior only within the accessible recording. Log provenance, continuity, timestamp/page locators and configuration. Record hidden operator actions and editing as limitations. Other people's hands-on tests are observational sources for a remote researcher.
- Firsthand testing requires actual access and documented trials; record firmware, participants, conditions and interventions. Never simulate physical access.

For signal/presence, actual participant responses or relevant published participant research are required for human interpretation claims. Spatial legibility requires evidence of bystander expectations, not just movement. Interaction fit needs intended-user/task evidence. Failure/recovery require actual breakdown sequences. Use not-assessed or insufficient with null scores when these are absent.

Repetition is study-specific: predeclare a justified trial plan for reliability claims, record every attempt and aborted trial, and do not manufacture rates, times or confidence.

## RXD-SC-001: Operating State Communication

Version: 1.0-draft. Dimension: signal. Review: draft.

### Research question

Can a person interpret operating state and intended action?

### Applicable categories

- Physical robots interacting with people

### Scenario and preconditions

- Document exact model, firmware, settings, operator experience and participant roles.
- Use an appropriate supervised environment and manufacturer-supported operations.
- Approach, pause and resume a task within the documented operating envelope.

### Procedure

- Record cues before each state transition and ask observers what they expect next.
- Define success and failure criteria before observation; record every attempt and any deviation.

### Observable behaviors

- Cue channel, timing and consistency
- participant interpretation with verbatim responses.

### Required evidence

- Source URL or durable record, access date, provenance, configuration and environment.
- Continuous footage or documented firsthand observations with event locators; identify edits and missing intervals.
- Independent evidence and human review before scoring. Manufacturer claims remain attributed claims.

### Minimum evidence

A state-transition sequence with context and participant interpretation. Timing claims need timestamped records.

### Measurement approach

Code observed behaviors against the stated question. Report participant accounts separately from evaluator inference. Quantitative measures require recorded trials, units and a documented analysis plan.

### Success criterion

The stated interaction objective is achieved in the defined context, supported by recorded behavior and participant interpretation where applicable.

### Failure criterion

The objective is not achieved, human interpretation conflicts with intended behavior, or intervention/abort is required. Record what happened without treating missing evidence as failure.

### Repetition requirements

Plan repeated trials before testing behavioral reliability. Record all attempts and interventions. Justify sample size for the research question; one success is not consistency.

### Environment

Document lighting, noise, space, surface, connectivity, crowding, language and accessibility needs. Compare models only under compatible conditions.

### Scoring criteria (draft)

- Preserved web endpoint: 1 — absent or unclear.
- Preserved web endpoint: 5 — exemplary, no instruction needed.
- Proposed intermediate anchors: 2 — substantial gaps in documented cases; 3 — objective met with identifiable gaps; 4 — objective met across documented cases with minor gaps. Editorial review required; not active scoring rules.

## RXD-RD-001: Recovery from Interrupted Tasks

Version: 1.0-draft. Dimension: recovery. Review: draft.
