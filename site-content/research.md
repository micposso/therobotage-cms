## Framework Feature — RXD

**Headline:** Robot Experience Design

**Description:** A six-dimension model for evaluating how robots perform in the environments they are deployed into — built for practitioners, not engineers.

**The Six Dimensions:**
1. Signal Clarity
2. Spatial Legibility
3. Perceived Presence
4. Failure Transparency
5. Interaction Fit
6. Recovery Design

**CTA:** Download white paper → /pdf/rxd-therobotage-v2.pdf *(internal PDF)*
