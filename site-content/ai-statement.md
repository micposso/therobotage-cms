## AI-Assisted Editorial

Large language models are used in drafting editorial content. All output goes through human editorial review before publication. AI-assisted copy is treated as a starting point, not a finished product.

---

## AI-Generated Images

Some images on the site are generated using AI tools. No AI-generated images incorporate third-party derivative works.

---

## What This Means for Readers

Commitment to transparency about AI involvement in production. Readers can trust that editorial positions and research findings are human-reviewed.
