## H1
Robots are entering homes, classrooms, and workplaces.

## Subtitle
A six-dimension model for evaluating robot interactions the way users actually experience them — built for practitioners, not engineers.

---

## The Six Dimensions

| # | Dimension | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Signal Clarity | How clearly a robot communicates its state, intent, and actions |
| 2 | Spatial Legibility | How well a robot's movement communicates its spatial intent |
| 3 | Perceived Presence | How a robot's embodiment affects the people around it |
| 4 | Failure Transparency | How robots communicate errors, limitations, and recovery |
| 5 | Interaction Fit | How well robot behaviour matches the expectations of the deployment context |
| 6 | Recovery Design | How systems handle failure and return to normal operation |
