## Why This Matters

### 1. Robots are arriving faster than the training
Deployment timelines outpace internal capability-building in most organisations.

### 2. Non-engineering staff carry the risk
The people closest to robots in daily operation rarely have structured preparation for it.

### 3. Readiness is a process, not a checklist
One-off briefings don't build lasting literacy. Structured programmes do.
