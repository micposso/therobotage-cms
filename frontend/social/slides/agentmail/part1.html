The instinct is to plug
robots into our tools.

A desktop robot lands on your desk. It has a voice, a camera, embodied intelligence running entirely on local models. The first thing everyone reaches for:

“Reachy, here’s access to my inbox.”
TheRobotAge

But access is not identity.

A robot reading your inbox is a feature hiding inside your account. That creates problems before it creates value:

No boundary

It can see every email you have, not just the ones meant for it.

No audit trail

Its actions blur into yours. You can’t tell what the robot did.

It acts as you

Anything it sends goes out under your name. A mistake is your mistake.

TheRobotAge

Give the robot its
own inbox.

AgentMail is email built for agents, not people. So Reachy gets its own address. Now it is not a feature inside your account. It is an entity that acts on its own behalf.

Access to your inbox
Its own inbox, its own identity,
its own audit trail.
