Did a robot help erase 17 World Cup goals?

That is the sharper question behind FIFA's semi-automated offside debate. A Canadian Premier League analysis said 17 goals disallowed for offside at the 2026 World Cup up to the Round of 32 would likely have stood under the daylight offside rule.

That does not mean VAR is literally a robot. It means football is now played inside robotic infrastructure: stadium-mounted cameras, connected ball data, state-estimation software, and human officials acting on machine-readable evidence.

The future of sport may not be robots replacing referees. It may be humans learning to perform under robotic perception.
