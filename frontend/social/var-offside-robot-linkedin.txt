VAR is not literally a robot. It is something more interesting: robotics as infrastructure.

FIFA's semi-automated offside system has no arms, legs, or wheels. It senses the field through 12 roof cameras, tracks up to 29 points per player at 50 Hz, reads the ball through a 500 Hz sensor, and sends decision support to human referees.

That makes VAR a useful case study in machine-readable environments. The stadium becomes the body: cameras act as eyes, the connected ball supplies timing data, software estimates state, and officials become the action layer.

Football is already training humans to play under sensor-assisted precision.
