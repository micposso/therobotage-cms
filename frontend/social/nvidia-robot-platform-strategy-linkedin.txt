NVIDIA's biggest robotics bet may be the infrastructure other robot makers depend on.

At GTC Berlin, Jensen Huang introduced Agile ONE SN, a humanoid reference platform developed with Agile Robots. The 1.4-meter, 40-kilogram robot brings together Jetson Thor computing, Isaac GR00T and dexterous hands. Expected in early 2027, it gives researchers an integrated starting point for developing and validating robotic skills.

For product designers and operations leads, the question extends beyond faster development. Shared tools could reduce duplicated engineering while increasing dependence on one provider's roadmap. And a capable research platform still leaves deployment teams responsible for how a robot communicates intent, handles interruptions and makes failures understandable to the people around it.
