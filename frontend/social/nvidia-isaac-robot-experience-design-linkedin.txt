The next robot design brief should include an interrupted handover.

Agile ONE SN, scheduled for early 2027, brings a reference humanoid together with NVIDIA Isaac GR00T and Jetson Thor. NVIDIA's expanded Isaac tools connect simulation, robot learning and runtime coordination. For product designers working with robotics engineers, that could mean more opportunities to test approach cues, pauses and recovery before committing to a deployment.

The distinction matters: completing a simulated task does not establish that an operator understands the robot. Planned Halos integration does not establish deployment-specific safety certification. Designers still need to define what the robot communicates when it stops, who can intervene and how work resumes.
