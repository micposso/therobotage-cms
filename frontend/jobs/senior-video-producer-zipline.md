## What the role actually is

Zipline is hiring a senior video producer on its communications and marketing side. The role is about making complex autonomous delivery work understandable through customer stories, launch narratives, service explainers, and public-facing video.

Video matters here because drone delivery is a physical experience. The person would help show how the system works, why communities should care, and what the service feels like when it reaches real customers.

## What you would work on

- Produce video stories for Zipline's communications, launches, and brand channels
- Translate autonomous logistics, customer impact, and community adoption into visual narratives
- Coordinate production plans, shoots, edits, reviews, and delivery across teams
- Work with communications, marketing, creative, operations, and local launch teams

## What they are asking for

- Video production experience for brand, communications, documentary, launch, or customer storytelling
- Strong editorial judgment and ability to simplify complex technical subjects
- Comfort managing production logistics, stakeholders, schedules, and approvals
- Interest in telling grounded stories about robotics, logistics, healthcare, retail, or public service

## Why this one is worth a look

Robotics companies need proof people can see. For Zipline, video can make the difference between an abstract drone-delivery claim and a service people understand. This role sits directly in that storytelling layer.
