## What the role actually is

Path Robotics is hiring a product manager for technical commercialization. The company builds AI-driven robotic welding systems, and this role is meant to turn complex robotics, AI, and software capabilities into customer-ready products.

The job sits at the zero-to-one product layer: discovery, feasibility, MVP, launch, adoption, and iteration. The listing specifically calls out functional, UX, and system-level requirements for robotic welding cycles and production environments.

## What you would work on

- Own greenfield product development from early discovery through MVP, commercialization, and launch
- Translate robotics, AI, and software capabilities into clear customer value propositions
- Define product strategy, success metrics, operating rhythms, and prioritization frameworks
- Represent customer needs through research, field insights, and manufacturing workflows
- Partner with sales, solutions, and operations on readiness, launch planning, enablement, and adoption

## What they are asking for

- Experience launching a greenfield product in robotics, manufacturing, automation, AI, or a related technical domain
- Technical product management or technical commercialization background
- Ability to bridge software, hardware, and data into production-ready solutions
- Comfort making decisions from customer insight, usage data, and experimentation
- Strong facilitation across engineering, sales, operations, and leadership

## Why this one is worth a look

Robotic welding is a useful test of product judgment because the robot has to survive real production constraints. This role is worth watching because it is about turning a hard technical capability into a repeatable commercial product, not just managing an existing roadmap.
