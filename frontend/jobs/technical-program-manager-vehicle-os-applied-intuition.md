## What the role actually is

Applied Intuition is hiring a technical program manager for Vehicle OS. The role sits between customers, subject-matter experts, proposals, project delivery, and the engineering work needed to make complex autonomy programs land.

This is not a pure roadmap PM position. It is a customer-facing technical program role for physical AI software where coordination, technical judgment, and implementation realism matter.

## What you would work on

- Lead technical pursuits and delivery plans for Vehicle OS customers
- Turn customer pain points into scoped proposals, milestones, and deliverables
- Coordinate with engineering, sales, and subject-matter experts
- Track program execution across requirements, timing, risk, and final outcomes

## What they are asking for

- Experience managing complex technical programs with customers
- Ability to understand autonomy, vehicle software, or adjacent embedded systems
- Strong cross-functional coordination with engineering and commercial teams
- Comfort owning both proposal work and delivery follow-through

## Why this one is worth a look

Vehicle operating systems are becoming a real product layer in autonomy. A TPM role here is interesting because the work is less about generic project tracking and more about making physical AI software deployable for demanding customers.
