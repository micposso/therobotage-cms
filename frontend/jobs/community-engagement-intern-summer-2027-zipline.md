## What the role actually is

Zipline is hiring a summer 2027 community engagement intern across U.S. launch markets including Texas, California, and Arizona. The intern would support local relationship-building, outreach, events, and education around autonomous delivery service launches.

This is not a technical robotics internship. It is a public-facing role for someone interested in how autonomous systems become acceptable, useful, and understandable in real communities.

## What you would work on

- Support community outreach for Zipline's U.S. autonomous delivery launches
- Help prepare materials, events, research, and relationship tracking for local stakeholders
- Work with community, policy, operations, and communications teams
- Capture questions and concerns from people affected by new delivery operations

## What they are asking for

- Interest in community engagement, communications, public affairs, policy, or local operations
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- Comfort working with civic, school, neighborhood, and business groups
- Ability to work onsite in one of the listed U.S. launch markets during the summer internship

## Why this one is worth a look

Zipline's drones are only part of the system. Community acceptance, public explanation, and local trust determine whether a launch works. This internship gives early-career candidates exposure to that side of robotics deployment.
