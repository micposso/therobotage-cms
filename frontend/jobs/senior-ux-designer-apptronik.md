## What the role actually is

Apptronik is hiring a senior UX designer for its Apollo humanoid platform. The role sits inside a human-centered robotics company bringing an embodied AI robot toward scaled use in manufacturing, logistics, and eventually broader settings.

The listing is less specific than the product manager postings, but the context is clear: the designer will be working on user experiences around a real humanoid platform, not a generic SaaS surface.

## What you would work on

- Design user experiences for Apollo and the product ecosystem around it
- Turn robotics workflows and technical constraints into clear interaction patterns
- Collaborate with product, engineering, and commercialization teams as Apollo moves toward scale
- Help make humanoid robot capabilities understandable to operators, customers, and internal teams

## What they are asking for

- Senior UX design experience for complex products
- Comfort with ambiguous, technical, multidisciplinary product environments
- Ability to communicate interaction ideas through prototypes, flows, and design artifacts
- Interest in embodied AI, humanoid robotics, manufacturing, or logistics use cases

## Why this one is worth a look

Humanoid UX is still early enough that the patterns are not settled. A senior designer at Apptronik will likely be deciding basic interaction conventions that become part of how customers understand Apollo, not just polishing screens after the product is defined.
