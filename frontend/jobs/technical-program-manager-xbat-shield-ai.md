## What the role actually is

Shield AI is hiring a technical program manager for the X-BAT organization. The role leads cross-functional integration, execution, and demonstration readiness across an autonomous aircraft development effort.

The posting is aimed at a technical program leader who can work across flight sciences, vehicle integration, aerostructures, autonomy, test, and operations while keeping milestones and risk reduction visible.

## What you would work on

- Lead technical execution and program coordination for X-BAT development work
- Align engineering, program, autonomy, test, and operations teams around demonstration objectives
- Track integration risks, readiness milestones, and cross-functional dependencies
- Use aerospace systems judgment to pressure-test plans and keep execution tied to technical reality

## What they are asking for

- Senior technical program management experience in aerospace, autonomy, or complex hardware systems
- Ability to operate across flight sciences, autonomy, vehicle integration, test, and operations teams
- Strong systems integration instincts and disciplined execution in rapid-iteration environments
- Comfort communicating risk, readiness, and tradeoffs to technical and program stakeholders

## Why this one is worth a look

X-BAT is a high-stakes autonomy program where product outcomes depend on hardware, autonomy, flight test, and demonstration timing all lining up. This role is worth tracking because it is program leadership at the boundary between aircraft development and autonomous behavior.
