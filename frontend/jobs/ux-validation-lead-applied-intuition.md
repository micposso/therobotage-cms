## What the role actually is

Applied Intuition is hiring a founding UX Validation Lead to build the bridge between HMI concepts and production software quality. The role defines test strategy, creates validation infrastructure, and develops a team around UX and HMI testing.

This is not a conventional qualitative research role. It is closer to a hands-on validation lead for interfaces where usability, automation, and product reliability all matter because the software is tied to vehicles and autonomy workflows.

## What you would work on

- Define end-to-end HMI and UX validation methods for Applied's products
- Build test approaches and automation infrastructure for interface quality
- Turn early HMI concepts into validated production-ready product surfaces
- Grow test engineers into domain specialists for UX and HMI validation
- Work across design, product, and engineering to close gaps before release

## What they are asking for

- Experience validating complex user interfaces, HMI systems, or safety-relevant software
- Ability to design test strategy and build practical validation infrastructure
- Comfort leading hands-on work while developing a team and practice area
- Strong collaboration with designers, engineers, product managers, and test specialists

## Why this one is worth a look

The most interesting part is the "founding" angle. Applied appears to be formalizing how it proves that autonomy interfaces are understandable and production-ready, which is a useful niche for someone who cares about the gap between UX intent and operational reality.
