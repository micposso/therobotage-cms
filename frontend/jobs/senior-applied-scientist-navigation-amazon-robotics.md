## What the role actually is

Amazon Robotics is hiring a senior applied scientist focused on robot navigation. The role combines learning-based planning, foundation models for embodied agents, and control-theoretic methods such as model-predictive trajectory planning.

The work is aimed at navigation systems that can move safely and fluidly through changing operational environments rather than only passing static benchmarks.

## What you would work on

- Research navigation systems that combine data-driven learning with control guarantees
- Develop planning and perception methods for dynamic robotics environments
- Connect academic advances in embodied AI with production-grade deployment needs
- Collaborate with robotics, software, and product teams on navigation capabilities and metrics

## What they are asking for

- Senior applied-science experience in robotics navigation, planning, control, or embodied AI
- Depth in learning-based planning, foundation models, or model-predictive control
- Ability to lead research toward robust real-world robotic deployment
- Communication skills for cross-functional work with scientists, engineers, and product managers

## Why this one is worth a look

Navigation is one of the places where autonomy becomes visible to nearby humans. A system can be technically capable and still feel unsafe or illegible if it moves poorly, so this is a research role with direct consequences for human-facing robot behavior.
