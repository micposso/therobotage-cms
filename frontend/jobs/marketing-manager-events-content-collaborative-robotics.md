## What the role actually is

Collaborative Robotics is hiring a hands-on marketing manager for events and content. The job centers on making physical AI understandable in the moments where people actually see it: trade shows, customer visits, partner meetings, investor demos, and the content that follows.

This is not a generic brand role. The person would coordinate marketing, GTM, engineering, design, and leadership to turn robot demonstrations into clear stories and measurable pipeline.

## What you would work on

- Plan and execute major external events and selected smaller activations
- Shape customer, partner, and investor visits around live robot demonstrations
- Draft event recaps, launch copy, technical explainers, and executive social posts
- Track event-sourced pipeline, audience growth, engagement, and content performance

## What they are asking for

- Experience producing events, campaigns, or content in a technical B2B environment
- Strong writing and project management across marketing, design, and technical teams
- Comfort owning logistics, vendors, budgets, run-of-show, and follow-up
- Ability to turn complex robot capabilities into stories non-builders can understand

## Why this one is worth a look

Cobot is building humanoid robots, which means the product often has to be believed in person before it is understood. This role sits right at that translation layer: the caveat is that it is onsite and execution-heavy, but the storytelling problem is unusually close to the actual robot.
