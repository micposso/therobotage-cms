## What the role actually is

Path Robotics is hiring a senior graphic designer for its in-house marketing function. The role produces social graphics, one-pagers, slide decks, web and email assets, trade show materials, print collateral, and data visuals for manufacturing and defense audiences.

The listing is clear that this is a hands-on individual contributor role: the seniority comes from judgment, craft, and the ability to turn technical material into credible design.

## What you would work on

- Design one-pagers, spec sheets, case studies, sales materials, and printed handouts
- Build decks for sales, executive presentations, conferences, and launches
- Create charts and simple data visualizations that stay accurate and on brand
- Produce social graphics, web assets, email visuals, and trade show materials
- Extend Path's brand system where dense robotics and industrial content needs clearer expression

## What they are asking for

- Senior-level graphic design craft with a strong portfolio
- Ability to turn sparse briefs, specs, or technical copy into polished visual work
- Comfort with dense industrial, manufacturing, defense, or technical content
- Hands-on production ability across digital, print, presentations, and event collateral
- Judgment to work directly with marketing leadership and ship quickly

## Why this one is worth a look

The best robotics marketing often depends on making complex machines and customer outcomes readable without sanding away the technical truth. This role is worth including because Path is asking for a designer who can make embodied-intelligence and welding-automation material precise, credible, and usable by commercial buyers.
