## What the role actually is

Boston Dynamics is hiring a research engineer for SLAM and spatial AI on the Atlas VLA research team. The role sits between real-time perception on humanoid hardware and offline reconstruction pipelines used to train and condition learned policies.

This is about giving Atlas a grounded 3D understanding of scenes, not just better image features.

## What you would work on

- Build real-time SLAM and perception-based state estimation for Atlas
- Develop 3D reconstruction pipelines from robot logs, teleoperation data, and multi-view geometry
- Connect geometric scene structure to VLM and VLA training workflows
- Prototype spatial AI methods that let learned policies reason about space

## What they are asking for

- Experience with SLAM, state estimation, 3D reconstruction, or spatial perception
- Ability to work across robotics, perception, ML, and systems software
- Comfort testing research code on advanced robotic hardware
- Interest in connecting classical geometric robotics with large learned models

## Why this one is worth a look

Humanoid autonomy will need more than end-to-end pixels if it is going to work in physical spaces people care about. This role is one of the more direct versions of that problem: building the spatial substrate that lets Atlas policies reason about where things actually are.
