## What the role actually is

Zipline is hiring a product manager for its Airspace Platform team. The role turns Zipline's production airspace-management experience and flight data into platform capabilities for airspace intelligence, coordination, interoperability, and expansion into new markets.

This is a product role at the intersection of autonomy, aviation, software, data, regulation, and real-world operations.

## What you would work on

- Define product strategy and roadmap for airspace intelligence, coordination, tracking, and operational workflows
- Turn real flight and airspace data into products that improve safety, visibility, efficiency, and access
- Work across autonomy, aviation, software, regulatory, and operations teams
- Shape how Zipline coordinates with other airspace users and external systems
- Move from ambiguous strategy to analytics, validation, and product decisions

## What they are asking for

- Product leadership on technically complex and ambiguous systems
- Ability to reason across software, data, aviation operations, and regulation
- Comfort using operational data to validate product direction
- Strong cross-functional execution in real-world, safety-sensitive environments

## Why this one is worth a look

The drone is only part of a delivery system; airspace coordination is where autonomy has to interoperate with the rest of the world. This role is worth tracking because it turns Zipline's operating history into a broader platform surface.
