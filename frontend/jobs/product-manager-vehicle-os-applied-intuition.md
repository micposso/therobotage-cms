## What the role actually is

Applied Intuition is hiring a product manager for Vehicle OS, the software layer around autonomous and software-defined vehicles. The role sits between customers, engineers, and internal product teams to define what the operating-system product needs to do in real deployments.

This is a technical product role for someone comfortable turning vehicle, autonomy, and customer requirements into roadmap choices. The work is less about a single app and more about the platform behavior that teams depend on to build, test, and operate autonomous systems.

## What you would work on

- Define product requirements and roadmap priorities for Vehicle OS capabilities
- Work with customers and field teams to translate deployment needs into product decisions
- Partner with engineering to make tradeoffs across platform, autonomy, and vehicle workflows
- Track adoption, quality, and release readiness for customer-facing product areas
- Communicate product strategy across internal teams and external stakeholders

## What they are asking for

- Product management experience with technical software, autonomy, automotive, or platform products
- Ability to work closely with engineering and customer teams on ambiguous requirements
- Strong judgment around roadmap scope, product quality, and release tradeoffs
- Comfort with in-person collaboration in Applied Intuition's Sunnyvale office

## Why this one is worth a look

Vehicle operating systems are where autonomy, controls, fleet workflows, and customer constraints start to become one product surface. This role is interesting because Applied sells into several physical-AI markets, so the product choices have to generalize across more than one vehicle program or demo.
