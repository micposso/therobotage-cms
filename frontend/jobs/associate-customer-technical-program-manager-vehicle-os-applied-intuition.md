## What the role actually is

Applied Intuition is hiring an associate customer technical program manager in Sunnyvale for Vehicle OS customer programs. The role supports in-vehicle infotainment work for a major automotive OEM, keeping the execution layer accurate across Jira, Confluence, release scope, defects, blockers, and customer reporting.

This is an early-career program role for someone who wants to grow into full ownership of technical customer programs while staying close to software releases, integration work, and customer-facing delivery.

## What you would work on

- Maintain program backlog health, release scope, labels, status, blockers, and dependency tracking in Jira
- Triage customer-reported defects and prepare recurring bug reviews with customer and engineering teams
- Produce weekly status reporting, delivery summaries, sprint showcase material, escalation trackers, and steering inputs
- Keep requirement mappings, decision logs, meeting notes, and process documentation current in Confluence
- Coordinate onsite integration, test bench, and bootcamp follow-ups across Applied and customer teams

## What they are asking for

- Early program, project management, engineering, technical consulting, or customer-facing technical experience
- Strong written communication and the judgment to escalate or close issues independently
- Comfort working directly with external customers, including when program news is difficult
- Technical literacy around software features, builds, releases, and integration issues
- Hands-on Jira and Confluence experience, or the ability to become fluent quickly

## Why this one is worth a look

This is a useful entry point into robotics-adjacent product execution because the work is not abstract coordination. The person is close to an OEM customer, Vehicle OS releases, integration details, and the operating habits that decide whether a complex physical-AI program stays on track.
