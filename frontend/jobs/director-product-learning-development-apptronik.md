## What the role actually is

Apptronik is hiring a director for product learning and development in Austin. The role appears focused on building the learning, training, and enablement layer around its humanoid products.

For a company commercializing Apollo, this kind of role matters because customers and internal teams need to understand what the robot can do, where it is limited, and how to use it responsibly.

## What you would work on

- Build learning and development programs connected to Apptronik's product organization
- Translate humanoid product knowledge into training, enablement, and operating materials
- Coordinate with product, engineering, commercial, and deployment teams
- Improve how internal and external users learn to work with Apollo

## What they are asking for

- Senior learning, enablement, product operations, or product education leadership experience
- Ability to structure complex technical knowledge for varied audiences
- Comfort working across robotics product, engineering, and go-to-market teams
- Strong judgment about how training supports safe and successful deployments

## Why this one is worth a look

Humanoid robotics adoption will depend on more than robot capability. The learning layer around the product is part of the user experience, especially when the system is new, physical, and unfamiliar to customers.
