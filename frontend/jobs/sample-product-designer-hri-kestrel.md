## What the role actually is

Sample listing for local development. You design the interaction layer between a mobile robot and the people sharing its floor: intent signaling, failure communication, and the handoff when a person has to intervene. Most of the design surface is not a screen.

## What you would work on

- Design the signaling vocabulary for intent, and validate that workers actually read it the way you intended
- Own the failure and recovery experience, which is where trust is won or lost
- Run studies on the floor with the people who work alongside the machine

## What they are asking for

- Has designed for physical products or environments, not only screens
- Runs research and can defend a finding that contradicts what the team wanted to hear
- Comfortable being the only designer in a room of engineers

## Why this one is worth a look

Very few robotics companies staff this role seriously, and the ones that do tend to produce noticeably better deployments. If you have been trying to move interaction design into physical systems, this is the shape of that job. Remote within the US, with regular travel to sites.
