## What the role actually is

Skydio is hiring a senior curriculum developer for sUAS operations. The role owns the instructional backbone of training programs: task analysis, learning objectives, assessments, certification paths, and sequencing across multiple delivery formats.

It is relevant to the board because it deals with the human operating model around autonomous drones, especially for critical industry users who need safe and repeatable workflows.

## What you would work on

- Define curriculum architecture for pilots, technicians, and internal teams
- Analyze operational tasks and turn them into measurable learning objectives
- Design assessments and certification paths for autonomous drone workflows
- Coordinate with instructors, product teams, simulation developers, and subject experts

## What they are asking for

- Curriculum development experience for technical or operational audiences
- Ability to translate complex systems into performance-based learning paths
- Comfort designing assessments, course maps, and certification structures
- Interest in autonomous drone operations and real-world user readiness

## Why this one is worth a look

As robotics products mature, training becomes part of the product surface. Skydio's curriculum role is a good example: the work shapes how real operators become competent with autonomous systems, not just how docs get written.
