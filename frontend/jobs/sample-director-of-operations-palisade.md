## What the role actually is

Sample listing for local development. You own supply chain, production planning, and the operations organization at a company whose processes were designed for a tenth of its current volume. The first year is largely replacing things that worked fine at the previous scale.

## What you would work on

- Rebuild demand planning and inventory management for a longer and more fragile component pipeline
- Manage the supplier relationships that gate the build schedule, especially on custom actuators
- Grow and structure an operations team that is currently organized by accident

## What they are asking for

- Has run operations through a hardware scaling transition before
- Deep supply chain experience with long-lead custom components
- Manages managers and has opinions about how to structure a team, not just staff one

## Why this one is worth a look

Robotics supply chains are unusually painful: low volumes, custom parts, and suppliers who do not prioritize you. This is a senior role with real authority at exactly the moment that authority matters. Onsite in Denver, which for an operations role is the right call.
