## What the role actually is

Agility is hiring a manager to run the Fremont data-collection operation that feeds robot-learning work. The job owns operator staffing, teleoperation station readiness, campaign execution, vendor management, and the quality metrics that decide whether AI teams get useful training data.

This is less a lab research job than the operating system around research data. The role matters because Agility's learned policies depend on consistent, high-quality episodes from real robot tasks.

## What you would work on

- Build, train, and manage a team of robot operators and data-collection specialists
- Keep teleoperation rigs, capture hardware, robots, and workcells ready for collection campaigns
- Track throughput, usable episode yield, station utilization, rig uptime, and cost per usable episode
- Coordinate vendors and contract labor against data quality and volume targets
- Turn task protocols and collection workflows into repeatable operating procedures

## What they are asking for

- Experience leading data collection, operations, production, test, or similar technical teams
- Comfort managing shift coverage, vendor performance, and operational metrics
- Ability to work closely with AI engineering on data quality and task coverage
- Interest in humanoid robots, teleoperation workflows, and learning systems

## Why this one is worth a look

Robot-learning progress depends heavily on the quality of the data operation behind it. Agility's posting is notable because it treats collection as a production function with measurable output, not a background support task.
