## What the role actually is

Kodiak is hiring a hardware technical program manager in Mountain View. The role owns planning and dependency tracking for hardware development across the company's industrial, over-the-road freight, and defense product lines.

This is not a classic product manager seat, but it is directly tied to product execution. The person would build roadmaps across engineering teams, manage hardware-platform integration, and keep development work aligned with program goals.

## What you would work on

- Create technical execution roadmaps across hardware and engineering teams
- Track feature dependencies, integration risks, and program milestones across product lines
- Manage development and integration work for Kodiak's current hardware platform
- Coordinate build progress so hardware execution stays aligned with industrial, freight, and defense objectives

## What they are asking for

- Technical program management experience with hardware, robotics, autonomy, automotive, or complex physical products
- Ability to coordinate engineering dependencies and turn them into execution roadmaps
- Comfort managing integration work where hardware stability and performance matter at fleet scale
- Willingness to work from Kodiak's Mountain View office with some travel

## Why this one is worth a look

Kodiak is trying to use the same autonomy foundation across commercial trucking and public-sector ground vehicles. That makes the hardware program layer more interesting than a narrow component role, because platform choices can affect multiple deployed product lines.
