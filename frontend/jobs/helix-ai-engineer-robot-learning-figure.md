## What the role actually is

Figure is hiring a robot learning engineer for Helix, its embodied AI effort for general-purpose humanoids. The role is centered on real-robot manipulation policies, from data collection through training, evaluation, deployment, and integration with the broader autonomy stack.

The work is explicitly practical robot learning. The listing emphasizes policies that survive sensor noise, partial observability, contact dynamics, and real-world variability rather than staying in simulation.

## What you would work on

- Design, train, evaluate, and deploy learned visuomotor policies for humanoid manipulation
- Develop behaviors such as grasping, pick-and-place, door opening, bimanual work, and assembly
- Apply behavior cloning, reinforcement learning, and VLA-style reasoning where useful
- Collaborate with perception, controls, systems, hardware, integration, and testing teams

## What they are asking for

- Hands-on robot learning experience on physical robots
- Background in manipulation, visuomotor control, and learning-based methods
- Ability to write production-quality Python or C++ for robotics and ML systems
- Experience designing experiments and evaluating model tradeoffs

## Why this one is worth a look

Figure is one of the clearest bets on humanoid robots as a general-purpose platform. This role sits at the uncomfortable but important edge between modern model training and a machine that has to touch real objects reliably.
