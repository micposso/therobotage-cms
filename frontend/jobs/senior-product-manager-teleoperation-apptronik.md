## What the role actually is

Apptronik is hiring a senior product manager to own teleoperation for Apollo. The product serves both internal data collection and customers trying to improve fleet performance, so the role spans operator experience, data throughput, interface quality, and safety.

The listing calls out VR or XR experience and human factors, which makes this more than a backlog-management role. The product surface is the place where a human operator, a robot body, and an autonomy data pipeline meet.

## What you would work on

- Own the teleoperation product roadmap and release priorities
- Define operator interfaces for teleoperated data collection and customer fleet support
- Balance data throughput, data quality, operator comfort, and safety
- Translate market needs into product requirements across the hardware/software divide

## What they are asking for

- Product management experience for technical, robotics, XR, or hardware/software products
- Human factors or operator-workflow sensibility
- Ability to define product vision and execute across engineering, design, and customer teams
- Experience with teleoperation, VR, XR, robotics data collection, or fleet operations

## Why this one is worth a look

Teleoperation is one of the hidden product surfaces in humanoid robotics. It shapes training data, customer support, and what the autonomy team can learn from the field. This role is compelling because operator experience directly affects the robot's learning loop.
