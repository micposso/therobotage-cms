## What the role actually is

Boston Dynamics is hiring a senior staff product marketing manager for Atlas in Waltham. The role owns announcements, go-to-market plans, product positioning, launch content, sales collateral, and customer-facing storytelling for the humanoid program.

The listing frames Atlas as moving from research visibility toward early real-world applications for manufacturers, with the marketing work tied closely to product, engineering, and sales.

## What you would work on

- Build the messaging and positioning for Atlas hardware, AI, and applications
- Plan Atlas announcements, launch campaigns, and go-to-market materials
- Create sales collateral, presentations, videos, case studies, webinars, and written content
- Partner with product, engineering, and commercial teams on customer-facing narratives

## What they are asking for

- Product marketing or product management experience for technical products
- Ability to turn robotics complexity into clear, credible stories
- Experience with major launches, campaigns, and buyer-journey content
- Comfort working across product, engineering, sales, and executive stakeholders

## Why this one is worth a look

Atlas is probably the most visible humanoid robot in the world, but visibility is not the same thing as product-market clarity. This role is interesting because it sits at the moment where Boston Dynamics has to explain what Atlas is for, who it helps, and why manufacturers should care.
