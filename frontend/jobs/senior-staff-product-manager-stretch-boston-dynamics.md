## What the role actually is

Boston Dynamics is hiring a senior staff product manager for Stretch, its autonomous mobile case-handling robot for warehouse logistics. The role is responsible for customer discovery, product strategy, roadmap decisions, and cross-functional launch work.

The posting emphasizes direct work with strategic partners to understand warehouse workflows, pain points, success criteria, and large-scale automation deployment constraints.

## What you would work on

- Translate warehouse customer needs into product requirements for engineering
- Shape roadmap priorities against market needs and technical feasibility
- Work with prospects and customers on workflows, usage data, and deployment goals
- Lead product lifecycle work from business case through launch

## What they are asking for

- Senior product management experience with complex technical systems
- Ability to turn customer workflows into clear engineering requirements
- Strength in market data, KPIs, financial models, and ROI analysis
- Robotics, logistics, or automation domain experience

## Why this one is worth a look

Stretch is one of Boston Dynamics' most commercially focused robots. This role is less about speculative robotics and more about whether a mobile manipulation product can solve hard warehouse work reliably enough to scale.
