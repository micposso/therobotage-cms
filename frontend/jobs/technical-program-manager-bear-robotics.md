## What the role actually is

Bear Robotics is hiring a technical program manager in Redwood City to support the CTO office across technical operations, project management, and strategic planning. The role sits close to engineering leadership, product milestones, technical documentation, and cross-functional execution.

The listing is partly executive-operations oriented, but it is still a robotics TPM role with product and technical delivery responsibilities. The person is expected to coordinate between departments, track decisions and action items, and work with teams distributed across California and Korea.

## What you would work on

- Coordinate technical requirements, project goals, and cross-team communication for the CTO office
- Track product and project milestones so technical work moves through execution cleanly
- Capture meeting decisions, follow up on action items, and keep technical records organized
- Support planning across a TPM team that spans U.S. and Korea time zones

## What they are asking for

- Background as a product manager or software engineer
- Familiarity with technical tools and software used in robotics environments
- Strong written and verbal communication with senior technical stakeholders
- High organization, discretion, and comfort working across multiple time zones

## Why this one is worth a look

Bear is deploying robots into people-heavy hospitality settings, where engineering decisions quickly become operational product decisions. This role has more CTO-office coordination than a classic product job, so it is best for someone who wants to sit near robotics leadership and keep complex technical programs moving rather than own a single product surface.
