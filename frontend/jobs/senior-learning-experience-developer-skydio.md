## What the role actually is

Skydio is hiring a senior learning experience developer for its training team. The role turns complex autonomous drone workflows into classroom, self-paced, simulation, and immersive learning experiences.

This is not a conventional UX research job, but it is squarely about the human side of robotics: how operators learn, trust, and safely use autonomous systems in the field.

## What you would work on

- Design learner-centered training experiences for pilots and technicians
- Translate drone systems and operational procedures into usable instruction
- Build media, simulation, and immersive training materials
- Partner with instructors, curriculum developers, product teams, and subject experts

## What they are asking for

- Learning experience design or instructional media experience
- Ability to make complex technical systems understandable to operators
- Comfort working across classroom, digital, simulation, and immersive formats
- Interest in improving field performance for autonomous drone users

## Why this one is worth a look

Robotics adoption often fails at the training layer. Skydio's role is interesting because it treats operator readiness as a product problem, especially as drone workflows become more autonomous and simulation-driven.
