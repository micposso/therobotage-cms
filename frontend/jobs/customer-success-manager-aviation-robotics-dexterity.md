## What the role actually is

Dexterity is hiring a customer success manager for aviation robotics, but the listing is clear that this is not a standard SaaS success role. The work is closer to customer-facing deployment program management: documentation, risk tracking, program plans, aviation compliance, and keeping technical teams and customers aligned.

The role supports robotics programs in airport and aviation environments, where the deployment work has to account for physical constraints, safety standards, and operational handoffs.

## What you would work on

- Coordinate program execution across engineering, product, operations, legal, supply chain, and customer teams
- Write and organize project plans, risk plans, concepts of operations, solution documents, and SOW material
- Translate technical issues into customer updates that non-technical stakeholders can understand
- Support site surveys and deployment handoffs for aviation robotics systems

## What they are asking for

- Program or project management experience in robotics, aerospace, or complex technology environments
- Strong writing and customer communication skills
- Experience with hardware, software, and real-world deployment programs
- Comfort with regulated or safety-critical environments

## Why this one is worth a look

Robotics adoption often fails in the space between a working demo and a usable customer program. This role lives in that gap. It is worth watching because aviation robotics will need people who can translate physical constraints, compliance, customer expectations, and engineering reality into a deployable operating plan.
