## What the role actually is

Zipline is hiring a Dallas-Fort Worth marketing manager to own market-level growth strategy for its drone delivery service. The role connects local customer behavior, commercial priorities, partnerships, and competitive dynamics into measurable marketing plans.

This is robotics go-to-market work close to deployment, where awareness, trust, merchant launches, and local adoption all affect whether a physical autonomous service takes hold.

## What you would work on

- Set the marketing strategy, priorities, and performance goals for the Dallas-Fort Worth market
- Build integrated plans across brand, lifecycle, partnerships, community engagement, merchant launches, and local activations
- Partner with commercial, operations, communications, community engagement, and central marketing teams
- Bring local customer insight and competitive learning back into broader Zipline planning

## What they are asking for

- Marketing, growth, brand, partnerships, or product marketing experience
- Experience building integrated marketing strategies that drive measurable business growth
- Strong analytical judgment for prioritizing local opportunities and measuring performance
- Ability to work cross-functionally without direct authority and operate onsite in Dallas-Fort Worth

## Why this one is worth a look

Local marketing for a delivery robot network is more operational than a standard SaaS growth role. The person has to understand customer demand, merchants, neighborhood behavior, and launch timing, which makes this a good fit for marketers who want to work where product adoption meets the real world.
