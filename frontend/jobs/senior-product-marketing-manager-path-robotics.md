## What the role actually is

Path Robotics is hiring a senior product marketing manager to own how its AI-driven robotic welding products are positioned for technical buyers. The role sits between product, sales, engineering, applications, and the market.

The listing is unusually direct: this person is expected to find gaps, build the materials sales needs, and make the company's messaging match what it actually builds. It is a product-marketing role for a robotics company selling into manufacturing environments with concrete operational pain.

## What you would work on

- Own positioning and messaging across Path's products and capabilities
- Translate robotics and AI capabilities into buyer-facing narratives
- Build sales enablement materials for technical manufacturing buyers
- Partner with product, engineering, applications, sales, and leadership
- Clarify market segments, customer pain points, launches, and competitive framing

## What they are asking for

- Product marketing experience for technical, industrial, AI, robotics, or manufacturing products
- Ability to turn complex capabilities into clear customer-facing positioning
- Strong sales enablement, launch, and go-to-market judgment
- Comfort working with product, engineering, sales, and customer-facing teams
- Bias toward owning ambiguous work without waiting for a detailed brief

## Why this one is worth a look

Product marketing in robotics is harder than turning specs into slides. This one is interesting because Path sells into shops that care about throughput, labor gaps, quality, and uptime, so the messaging has to translate embodied AI into plain operational value.
