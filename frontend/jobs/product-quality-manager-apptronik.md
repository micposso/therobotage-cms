## What the role actually is

Apptronik is hiring a product quality manager for Apollo, its humanoid robot platform. The role documents build and system-test issues, drives corrective action across hardware, operations, and software, and defines product-quality plans as the company moves from prototypes toward production.

This is not a generic quality role. It sits close to product maturity for a humanoid robot that has to move from internal development into repeatable manufacturing and customer-facing reliability.

## What you would work on

- Lead product-quality processes across builds, system testing, and production readiness
- Document issues, align stakeholders, and drive root-cause and corrective action
- Build dashboards and visual tools that make top issues visible to leadership
- Define quality plans for contract manufacturing and audit adherence
- Coordinate quality work across hardware design, operations, software, and manufacturing

## What they are asking for

- Product quality experience on complex technical or electromechanical products
- Ability to lead quality team members and cross-functional issue resolution
- Strong critical thinking, ownership, and communication under ambiguity
- Experience turning build and test findings into durable process improvements
- Comfort working with hardware, software, operations, and manufacturing stakeholders

## Why this one is worth a look

Humanoid startups are now hitting the manufacturing and serviceability problems that decide whether a platform becomes a product. Apptronik's role is a useful signal that Apollo's next challenge is not only capability, but product quality at scale.
