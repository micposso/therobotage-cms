## What the role actually is

Shield AI is hiring a TPM to lead Hivemind autonomy integration onto X-BAT. The role coordinates aircraft engineering, autonomy software, manufacturing, lab availability, flight test, and operational teams.

This is a senior hardware/software program role where mission autonomy has to become an aircraft capability, not just a software roadmap.

## What you would work on

- Drive the integration program for Hivemind autonomy on X-BAT
- Align software development with aircraft hardware, manufacturing, lab, and flight-test schedules
- Define program objectives with product, engineering, operations, and executive stakeholders
- Manage scope, schedule, budget, risk, and technical performance through deployment

## What they are asking for

- Senior experience leading complex cross-functional engineering programs
- Technical understanding of software-enabled systems, autonomy, aerospace, or integrated products
- Experience coordinating programs from concept through production and operational deployment
- Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. Secret clearance

## Why this one is worth a look

Mission autonomy roles like this sit at the handoff from AI capability to deployed system. The compensation range is high, and so is the coordination burden: this is for someone who can keep autonomy, aircraft, and defense-program constraints moving together.
