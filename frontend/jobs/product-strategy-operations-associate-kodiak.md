## What the role actually is

Kodiak is hiring a product strategy and operations associate to work between product, engineering, and operations. The role turns messy questions about how autonomous trucks are built, deployed, and scaled into analyses, recommendations, and operating rhythms.

This is a closer-to-product strategy role rather than a pure business operations job. The person would sit with the teams building the autonomous trucking product and help shape decisions that affect deployment readiness.

## What you would work on

- Scope ambiguous cross-functional problems into clear analyses and recommendations
- Drive product, engineering, and operations decisions from analysis through execution
- Build operating cadences, metrics, and dashboards for autonomy deployment work
- Help leadership understand tradeoffs across product readiness, field operations, and scale
- Turn deployment lessons into clearer process and product priorities

## What they are asking for

- Strategy, operations, consulting, product operations, or similar analytical experience
- Strong structured problem-solving and comfort with ambiguous technical programs
- Ability to work closely with product, engineering, and operations leaders
- Clear written and verbal communication for decision-making
- Interest in getting close to autonomous-vehicle product development

## Why this one is worth a look

Kodiak is in the hard middle of autonomy commercialization: not just proving a truck can drive, but deciding what needs to be true for a fleet to scale. This role is interesting because it sits at that product operating layer where field reality turns into roadmap and execution choices.
