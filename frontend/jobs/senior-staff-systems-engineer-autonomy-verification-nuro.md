## What the role actually is

Nuro is hiring a senior or staff systems engineer for autonomy verification. The role owns requirements, metrics, acceptance criteria, and test strategy for proving the Nuro Driver can operate safely in its target operating design domain and follow rules of the road.

This belongs on the board because it is evaluation work for robot behavior: deciding what evidence is required before autonomy can ship.

## What you would work on

- Create requirements for safe autonomous operation within Nuro's target ODD
- Design metrics and acceptance criteria for autonomy behavior and rules-of-road compliance
- Use simulation, logs, closed-course tests, and accident reconstructions to verify requirements
- Collaborate with behavior, perception, data science, product, legal, and public trust teams
- Own the verification strategy from requirements definition through evidence generation

## What they are asking for

- Experience developing or validating autonomous systems
- Ability to define requirements, metrics, and acceptance criteria for complex behavior
- Familiarity with safety standards, testing methodologies, simulation, or autonomy evaluation
- Strong cross-functional collaboration with autonomy software, simulation, product, and operations teams
- Systems-level judgment about what makes robot behavior deployable

## Why this one is worth a look

Autonomy evaluation is where product claims meet proof. Nuro's role is notable because it treats behavior metrics and verification strategy as first-class work, not an afterthought once the autonomy stack is built.
