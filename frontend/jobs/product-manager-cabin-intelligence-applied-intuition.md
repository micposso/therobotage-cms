## What the role actually is

Applied Intuition is hiring a product manager for Cabin Intelligence inside its Vehicle Intelligence work. The role owns the product platform, tooling, and AI-powered in-vehicle experiences that automakers can take from early proof of concept through production.

This is a product role at the intersection of autonomy-adjacent software, automotive UX, and OEM delivery. The person would work across engineering, design, business development, marketing, customers, and technology partners.

## What you would work on

- Define the roadmap for Cabin Intelligence platform capabilities, tools, and applications
- Ship production-grade in-vehicle experiences while balancing platform reuse with OEM-specific needs
- Work with engineering and design on priorities from early concepts through start of production
- Own technology partner decisions around AI models, voice, content, and system-on-chip vendors
- Gather customer feedback from OEMs and turn it into product and go-to-market decisions

## What they are asking for

- Product management experience in a technical company or high-growth startup
- Experience with automotive, trucking, OEM, supplier, or vehicle software products
- Ability to work with engineering, design, customer, and business teams on product tradeoffs
- Comfort owning product strategy, launch execution, partnerships, and customer feedback loops

## Why this one is worth a look

Cabin Intelligence is a useful lens on where physical AI shows up for ordinary users: inside the vehicle, through voice, perception, personalization, and driver or passenger workflows. The role is not robotics in the narrow warehouse sense, but it is very much about translating machine intelligence into a product experience people can understand and trust.
