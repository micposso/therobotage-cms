## What the role actually is

Boston Dynamics is hiring a staff ML research scientist for its Machine Learning Safety R&D team. The role is about models that help robots detect hazards, understand humans and environmental features, and behave more safely in shared spaces.

For this board, the relevance is the human-aware robotics angle: the work connects ML architecture choices to how robots operate around people.

## What you would work on

- Research model architectures for robots operating safely near people
- Build and validate ML models for hazards, humans, and environmental understanding
- Develop datasets, metrics, and validation plans for safety-focused ML research
- Prototype perception and behavior models with a small cross-functional team
- Carry models through research, deployment, and online validation loops

## What they are asking for

- Deep learning experience on scalable real-world computer vision, LLM, or robotics problems
- Experience across the ML lifecycle from network design to deployment and validation
- Familiarity with modern architectures such as transformers, VLMs, VLAs, or deep reinforcement learning
- Ability to connect research choices to real robotic safety requirements
- Eligibility to work in the United States

## Why this one is worth a look

Safety in robotics is often treated as compliance or testing, but this role frames it as a model-design problem. That makes it especially relevant as robots move from controlled workcells into spaces where human behavior is a core part of the environment.
