## What the role actually is

Boston Dynamics is hiring a staff TPM to manage the resources behind its AI teams. The role owns sequencing and prioritization across training clusters, data processing pipelines, and data operations so robotics teams can move faster.

This is not a model-building role. It is a program role around the scarce infrastructure that determines which robotics AI work gets done first.

## What you would work on

- Prioritize training cluster, data pipeline, and data operations work
- Coordinate dependencies across engineering, data, and infrastructure teams
- Build resource plans for competing AI robotics initiatives
- Keep leadership and technical stakeholders aligned on tradeoffs and outcomes

## What they are asking for

- Technical program management experience in complex engineering environments
- Ability to reason about compute, data, and infrastructure constraints
- Strong dependency management across multiple technical teams
- Comfort balancing strategic planning with day-to-day execution

## Why this one is worth a look

AI robotics progress is increasingly gated by data and compute orchestration, not just model ideas. This role is worth watching because it shows Boston Dynamics formalizing that operational layer around its robotics AI strategy.
