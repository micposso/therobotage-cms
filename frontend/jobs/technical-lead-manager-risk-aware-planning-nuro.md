## What the role actually is

Nuro is hiring a technical lead manager for risk-aware planning in Mountain View. The team owns robotics-based safety protections for the autonomy stack and the loop for diagnosing and resolving real-world autonomy issues.

This is a research-adjacent autonomy role because the work connects planning methods, safety constraints, field behavior, evaluation, and deployment quality.

## What you would work on

- Own core logic and implementation for robotics-based safety protections in the autonomy stack
- Lead root-cause analysis and principled fixes for real-world autonomy issues
- Grow and guide a technical team working on safety-critical planning behavior
- Work with AI modeling, evaluation, systems, and operations teams on deployment-quality autonomy

## What they are asking for

- Deep experience with safety-critical motion planning, decision making, trajectory optimization, or controls for real-world robots or autonomous vehicles
- Strong technical judgment across C++, geometry, optimization, probability, physics, and planning methods
- Experience taking robot behavior from development into real-world operation
- Hands-on leadership ability for a team working under high safety and execution standards

## Why this one is worth a look

Nuro's autonomy platform has to combine data-driven behavior with principled safety protections. That makes this role interesting for people who want to work on the boundary between autonomy research, production planning systems, and the messy issue loop that appears only after vehicles operate in the world.
