## What the role actually is

Applied Intuition is hiring a cross-functional events producer to own a growing event footprint. The role sits between marketing strategy, executive communications, vendor logistics, and hands-on execution.

This is not a generic events coordinator job. The listing includes customer events, investor events, press activations, product showcases, demo environments, conference booths, and executive-level programming across defense, automotive, industrial, and trucking verticals.

## What you would work on

- Advise on event strategy across industry conferences, partner moments, and vertical marketing plans
- Run executive events, customer events, investor days, press activations, and sales presentations
- Own programming, run of show, speaker coordination, itineraries, and day-of execution
- Partner with design and web teams on invitations, registration, booths, collateral, and swag
- Oversee demo environments and product showcases so technical presentations land cleanly

## What they are asking for

- Events experience in complex, executive-facing, or technical environments
- Familiarity with industry conferences, media events, partner summits, and investor days
- Strong vendor, logistics, programming, and cross-functional execution skills
- Ability to manage high-visibility internal and external communications
- Comfort operating with autonomy and a high attention to detail

## Why this one is worth a look

Robotics and physical AI sell through moments where people see, touch, and question the system. That makes events more central than they are in many software categories. This role is about making demos, customer rooms, and executive moments feel credible.
