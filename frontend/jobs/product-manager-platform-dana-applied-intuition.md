## What the role actually is

Applied Intuition is hiring a product manager for the core platform behind Dana, its AI platform for physical industries. The role owns the customer-facing builder, deployment, sharing, and governance experience for enterprise users.

This is a platform PM job where product quality, user experience, multi-tenancy, scale, reliability, onboarding, and security review all matter.

## What you would work on

- Define and ship core capabilities for builders ranging from platform engineers to domain experts
- Own product quality and user experience across the Dana platform
- Partner with platform engineering on architecture, scale, reliability, and multi-tenant tradeoffs
- Work with forward-deployed teams and design partners to understand customer needs
- Join enterprise onboarding and security-review conversations when they affect the product

## What they are asking for

- Product management experience on a cloud or SaaS product shipped to external customers
- Ownership of platform capabilities such as integrations, identity, permissions, or hosting
- Technical fluency with architecture tradeoffs and multi-tenant systems
- Enterprise exposure around security, admin, or IT requirements
- Product taste for how the tool feels to the person using it

## Why this one is worth a look

This is not a robot-interface role, but it is squarely in the infrastructure that lets physical AI teams build and govern tools. It is a strong fit for PMs who like platform work but want the product context to be machines in the world.
