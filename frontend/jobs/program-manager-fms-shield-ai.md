## What the role actually is

Shield AI is hiring a program manager for Foreign Military Sales programs tied to its V-BAT business. The role runs execution from contract award through delivery, acceptance, training, sustainment, and follow-on support.

This is a customer-facing program role for deployed autonomous aircraft systems. The person would coordinate government program offices, international customers, engineering, operations, logistics, and sustainment teams.

## What you would work on

- Lead FMS program execution across delivery, training, fielding, sustainment, and acceptance
- Manage U.S. Government and international customer relationships for assigned programs
- Track cost, schedule, resource, and performance metrics against contract requirements
- Identify bottlenecks and drive critical-path decisions to keep programs on schedule

## What they are asking for

- Department of Defense program management experience
- Ability to manage multiple interrelated projects with limited direction
- Familiarity with scheduling tools, complex project financials, and customer reporting
- Ability to obtain a Secret security clearance and travel domestically or internationally

## Why this one is worth a look

Program roles like this decide whether autonomous systems move from demo to accepted field capability. The caveat is that the work is defense-procurement heavy, but it is close to the real-world adoption loop for robotic aircraft.
