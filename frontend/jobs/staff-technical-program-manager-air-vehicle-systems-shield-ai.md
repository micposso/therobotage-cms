## What the role actually is

This Staff TPM role plans and coordinates air-vehicle systems work for Shield AI's next-generation autonomous aircraft. It is a hardware-centric program role spanning subsystem leads, integration, verification, suppliers, and flight-readiness milestones.

## What you would work on

- Build integrated schedules and execution plans for aircraft subsystems and vehicle-level milestones
- Coordinate across flight controls, avionics, electrical power, thermal systems, structures, manufacturing, supply chain, and flight test
- Track interfaces, requirements, verification plans, supplier deliverables, and cross-system risks
- Translate subsystem status into clear decisions for engineering, program, and executive stakeholders

## What they are asking for

- Experience in technical program management, systems engineering, or engineering leadership for aerospace or complex hardware
- Working understanding of subsystem interfaces, design maturation, verification, hardware testing, and integration
- Ability to manage schedules, decisions, dependencies, technical risk, and supplier execution
- Clear communication with responsible engineers, technical leads, and program leadership

## Why this one is worth a look

The listing is deeply technical, but the role is about making autonomous aircraft development legible and executable across many teams. It fits the board because it is a program/product execution seat for a physical autonomy platform, not a narrow component-design job.
