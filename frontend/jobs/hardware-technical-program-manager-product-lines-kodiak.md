## What the role actually is

Kodiak is hiring a technical program manager for the hardware platform work behind its autonomous ground transportation products. The role connects engineering roadmaps, build schedules, platform integration, and product-line dependencies across commercial trucking, industrial, and defense programs.

This is a TPM seat for physical product execution. The person would keep hardware, software, systems, and operations teams aligned as Kodiak scales its current platform.

## What you would work on

- Create technical execution roadmaps across engineering teams and product lines
- Manage development and integration work for Kodiak's current hardware platform
- Track schedules, milestones, dependencies, resources, and build progress
- Resolve risks across hardware, software, systems, and operations deliverables

## What they are asking for

- Technical program management experience with complex hardware or robotics programs
- Ability to turn cross-functional dependencies into realistic schedules and milestones
- Comfort communicating program status and tradeoffs across seniority levels
- Willingness to work from Kodiak's Mountain View office with some travel

## Why this one is worth a look

Kodiak's autonomy stack now spans freight and defense use cases, so hardware program work has direct consequences for what can actually be deployed. The caveat is that this is not a discovery-oriented product manager role; it is for someone who likes the hard mechanics of getting physical platforms integrated and shipped.
