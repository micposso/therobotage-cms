## What the role actually is

Shield AI is hiring a TPM for mission autonomy inside its aircraft work. The role leads integration of the Hivemind autonomy stack onto next-generation aircraft platforms from concept through production and operational deployment.

This is a hardware/software integration program role. The person would be a coordination point between aircraft engineering, autonomy software teams, and operational stakeholders.

## What you would work on

- Lead aircraft and autonomy integration programs for Hivemind-equipped platforms
- Coordinate hardware, autonomy software, and operational teams through the product lifecycle
- Track schedule, cost, technical performance, integration readiness, and operational outcomes
- Keep distributed teams aligned across aircraft and autonomy development

## What they are asking for

- Technical program management experience with complex hardware/software integration
- Comfort coordinating autonomy software and aircraft engineering work
- Strong planning discipline around schedule, cost, technical risk, and readiness
- Ability to work with distributed teams across multiple U.S. sites

## Why this one is worth a look

This role is close to the point where autonomy stops being a lab stack and becomes an aircraft program. It is a fit for someone who wants mission autonomy work with hard integration, schedule, and deployment constraints.
