## What the role actually is

Kodiak is hiring a world model research scientist for physical AI in Mountain View. The role points at model-based understanding of driving scenes and autonomy behavior for a self-driving trucking platform.

World models matter because they can change how autonomous systems predict, plan, simulate, and evaluate what happens around them.

## What you would work on

- Research world-model approaches for autonomous driving and physical AI
- Build experiments that connect perception, prediction, planning, and simulation
- Evaluate model behavior against real-world autonomy needs
- Collaborate with autonomy and product teams on how research informs the driving stack

## What they are asking for

- Research depth in machine learning, autonomy, robotics, or world models
- Experience with large-scale model training, simulation, or embodied AI evaluation
- Ability to turn research ideas into working autonomy experiments
- Strong communication across research and engineering teams

## Why this one is worth a look

Kodiak has a focused autonomous trucking domain rather than a generic robotics platform. That makes the world-model work more grounded: success depends on whether the research improves a vehicle that has to operate in high-stakes logistics environments.
