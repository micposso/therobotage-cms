## What the role actually is

Applied Intuition is hiring a senior ML engineer for the generative-modeling side of its Neural Simulation product. The work focuses on diffusion and video generation models that turn real-world driving data into controllable, photorealistic simulation scenarios.

The role sits at the research-to-product boundary: improving realism, temporal consistency, controllability, and evaluation workflows so autonomy teams can train and validate systems against harder simulated conditions.

## What you would work on

- Develop diffusion and video-generation models for driving-scene simulation
- Generate sensor data conditioned on scene layout, camera pose, actors, and trajectories
- Augment real-world logs with new scenarios, actors, weather, and lighting conditions
- Define metrics and benchmarks for realism, temporal consistency, controllability, and sim-to-real gap
- Work with autonomy, infrastructure, research, and customer-facing teams to productionize new methods

## What they are asking for

- Strong ML experience in generative modeling, computer vision, or related areas
- Ability to bring recent research into production systems
- Experience with large-model training or inference workflows
- Interest in autonomy simulation and evaluation for physical AI systems

## Why this one is worth a look

Simulation quality is becoming a core constraint for autonomy teams. This role is interesting because it applies fast-moving generative-model research to concrete validation problems for vehicles and other moving machines.
