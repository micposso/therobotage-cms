## What the role actually is

Applied Intuition is looking for a community-focused social operator for its technical audience. The role owns the daily publishing and engagement engine across channels like LinkedIn and X while working with social, writing, design, and subject-matter teams.

The core problem is credibility. Applied wants someone who can participate in conversations around autonomy, simulation, foundation models, and physical AI without making the company sound generic or detached from the developer community.

## What you would work on

- Publish and adapt approved content across Applied's owned social channels
- Keep the content queue, social calendar, and asset trackers current
- Respond to comments and direct messages from technical audiences
- Identify recurring community voices, topic trends, and conversations worth joining
- Coordinate with marketers, writers, designers, and technical experts on daily execution

## What they are asking for

- Community management or social media experience with a community-facing role
- Ability to write and adapt content for technical and developer audiences
- Familiarity with social listening, engagement patterns, and channel operations
- Strong organization across calendars, queues, assets, and stakeholders
- Interest in developer, open-source, employer-brand, or technical-marketing communities

## Why this one is worth a look

The useful version of robotics marketing now happens in public technical channels, not only in polished launches. This role is a fit for someone who likes the daily conversation layer where engineers, researchers, and prospective customers decide whether a company is actually worth following.
