## What the role actually is

Path Robotics is hiring a creative content producer for a small in-house creative team. The role owns photo, video, motion, design, and campaign assets across product, customer, event, people, and brand work.

This is a hands-on creative role that can grow into broader brand stewardship, but the listing is clear that the person still needs to make the work.

## What you would work on

- Capture photo and video for product, customer, company, and event marketing
- Lead shoots at events, customer locations, field installations, and HQ
- Edit, color grade, and prepare deliverables for web, print, social, and press
- Develop motion graphics, light VFX, banners, decks, one-pagers, and trade show graphics
- Repurpose existing creative into cutdowns, GIFs, reels, and launch assets
- Keep creative aligned with Path's visual identity and organized for reuse

## What they are asking for

- Strong portfolio-backed photo and video production experience
- Ability to own creative from concept through final delivery
- Fluency with Adobe tools including video, design, and motion workflows
- Comfort working across marketing, operations, launches, and robotics customer environments

## Why this one is worth a look

Robotics companies need credible visual storytelling because the product is physical and unfamiliar. This role is relevant to the board because it is not generic content work; it is about making embodied AI and industrial robot deployments legible.
