## What the role actually is

Nuro is hiring a technical lead manager for autonomy evaluation and intelligence. The role owns tooling and methods that validate the Nuro Driver, including smart evaluation agents, intelligent metrics, and feedback loops that connect simulation, road data, and model behavior.

This is an engineering leadership role, but its subject is directly relevant to robotics research: how a team decides whether an autonomous system is behaving well enough to deploy.

## What you would work on

- Set the technical roadmap for autonomy evaluation and validation systems
- Build or guide intelligent agents and metrics for complex driving interactions
- Use evaluation frameworks to close the loop between model behavior and system improvement
- Lead a team working at the boundary of simulation, ML, and real-world autonomy

## What they are asking for

- Leadership depth in autonomy, ML evaluation, simulation, robotics, or related systems
- Ability to design metrics that measure behavior quality, not just component accuracy
- Experience guiding technical teams through ambiguous research and deployment problems
- Comfort working in Mountain View with autonomy, safety, and systems teams

## Why this one is worth a look

Robotics hiring often talks about model capability, but evaluation is where the truth shows up. This role is compelling because it focuses on the intelligence-measurement layer that decides whether autonomy is improving in ways people can trust.
