## What the role actually is

Shield AI is hiring a product designer for its Ground Control Station team. The role focuses on UX for software that lets operators plan, command, and monitor autonomous unmanned aircraft in real-time environments.

This is not a general product-design posting with robotics language attached. The listing calls out spatial interfaces, operator confidence, mission-critical workflows, and direct collaboration with engineers, operators, and product teams.

## What you would work on

- Shape UX for next-generation UAS ground control software
- Design workflows for mission planning, command, monitoring, and autonomous-asset awareness
- Build spatial interfaces that help operators act with confidence and clarity
- Work alongside engineers, operators, and product teams on real-time control problems

## What they are asking for

- Product design experience in complex technical software
- Systems thinking for real-time, mission-critical workflows
- Ability to collaborate with engineering and operator stakeholders
- Interest in autonomous aircraft, ground control stations, or related control software

## Why this one is worth a look

Autonomous aircraft UX is a trust and control problem under pressure. This role is worth tracking because it asks a designer to make autonomous behavior understandable enough for operators who may be making fast, consequential decisions.
