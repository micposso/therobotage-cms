## What the role actually is

Figure is hiring an NPI engineer to run prototype and pilot production builds for its humanoid robots. The job is about manufacturing readiness: materials, build schedules, change impact, ECNs, issue tracking, root-cause follow-up, and keeping cross-functional teams aligned during early production.

This is the kind of role that turns an impressive lab machine into something a company can build again and again.

## What you would work on

- Own MBOM accuracy and readiness for prototype and pilot builds
- Coordinate material arrival, staffing, schedules, and build execution
- Track ECNs, design changes, build issues, root causes, and corrective actions
- Keep engineering, manufacturing, supply chain, and production stakeholders moving toward build milestones

## What they are asking for

- Experience in NPI, manufacturing, supply chain, or project management
- Strong understanding of BOM management, manufacturing processes, and build schedules
- Comfort with PLM, ERP, MRP, scheduling, or data analysis tools
- Communication and leadership skills for driving accountability across teams

## Why this one is worth a look

Humanoid robotics has a manufacturing credibility problem: the category will be judged by whether companies can build reliable units at meaningful volume, not only by whether the videos look good. This role is right at that transition from prototype to production system.
