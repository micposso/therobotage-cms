## What the role actually is

Amazon Robotics is hiring a technical product manager for the GO-AI Technology and Development team. The role owns roadmap and feature strategy across annotation tools, near-real-time remote intervention, data discovery, reporting, and foundation-model workflows for robotics operations.

The interesting piece is the shift from manual visual reasoning work toward model-assisted automation and teleoperation. This is product management for the systems that help Amazon Robotics scale AI-backed operational decisions.

## What you would work on

- Define product strategy for GO-AI applications and data workflows
- Translate robotics operations needs into roadmap priorities and technical proposals
- Shape new capabilities for remote real-time teleoperation
- Work with engineering, science, and operations teams on model-assisted annotation and intervention systems

## What they are asking for

- Product management experience for technical or AI-heavy systems
- Ability to work with applied science and engineering teams on architecture tradeoffs
- Comfort with robotics, fulfillment operations, annotation, labeling, or foundation-model workflows
- Data-driven judgment about customer needs, operations, and product execution

## Why this one is worth a look

This role sits at a useful layer of robotics product work: not the robot hardware itself, but the AI operations surface that decides how quickly robots can learn from edge cases and human intervention. It is a strong fit for someone interested in product work around embodied AI infrastructure.
