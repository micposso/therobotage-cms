## What the role actually is

Sample listing for local development. You manage the teleoperation team and the pipeline that turns their sessions into training data. The interesting problem is not the tooling, it is that inconsistent operators produce inconsistent demonstrations and the model learns the inconsistency.

## What you would work on

- Build the review process that catches a low-quality demonstration before it reaches the training set
- Set the operator training and calibration standard, and measure whether it holds
- Own the pipeline from raw session capture through labeled, versioned dataset

## What they are asking for

- Has managed an operations team where output quality was measurable and contested
- Comfortable enough with data tooling to argue with the ML team on the merits
- Has thought seriously about the working conditions of remote operators

## Why this one is worth a look

Teleoperation is the least discussed and most load-bearing part of most autonomy programs, and roles that own it end to end are rare. This one is fully remote within the US. It is also a role where the people-management half is real, not nominal.
