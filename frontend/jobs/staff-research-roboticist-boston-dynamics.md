## What the role actually is

Boston Dynamics is hiring a staff research roboticist for the Spot team. The role develops learning-based perception and control methods for last-mile delivery, then validates them in simulation and on physical robots.

This is a robotics research role with a direct deployment loop, not a disconnected modeling seat.

## What you would work on

- Design, train, and deploy ML and reinforcement-learning algorithms for Spot
- Test algorithms in simulation and on real robots in unstructured outdoor settings
- Build Python and C++ software that can survive hardware integration and field evaluation
- Translate research ideas into capabilities for delivery workflows

## What they are asking for

- Graduate-level robotics, machine learning, computer science, or related background
- Experience deploying ML or RL techniques onto physical robotic hardware
- Practical depth with modern ML frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, or JAX
- Strong software engineering habits in Python or C++

## Why this one is worth a look

Spot delivery work is a useful signal that legged robots are being pushed toward messier logistics tasks outside controlled demos. The caveat is that this is a senior research-and-deployment role: the interesting part is the same thing that makes it hard.
