## What the role actually is

Applied Intuition is hiring a product manager for developer tooling inside its Vehicle OS work. The role owns a product end to end, from strategy and market understanding through engineering execution, launch, customer feedback, and go-to-market support.

This is a technical product role for someone who can bridge autonomy engineers, customers, business development, and marketing.

## What you would work on

- Define platform products from market understanding, customer use cases, and time-to-market needs
- Shape a differentiated value proposition for autonomy developer tools
- Partner closely with engineering to prioritize work and ship useful product increments
- Manage the product lifecycle from discovery through delivery and business outcome
- Work with business development and marketing on pitches, pricing, positioning, and engagement models

## What they are asking for

- Product management experience in a top technology company or high-growth startup
- Experience launching products, ideally involving both software and hardware
- Strong technical background and comfort with software development concepts
- Ability to own product strategy, execution, customer feedback, and lifecycle management

## Why this one is worth a look

Developer tooling is where autonomy products either become easier to build or stay trapped inside bespoke engineering workflows. This role is useful for a PM who wants a software product surface but still wants the constraints of autonomous systems.
