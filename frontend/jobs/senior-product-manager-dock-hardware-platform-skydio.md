## What the role actually is

Skydio is hiring a senior PM for the dock and drone ecosystem around its X10 platform. The role owns product strategy and delivery across hardware and software, with a focus on making autonomous drone systems easier to deploy, maintain, and improve over time.

## What you would work on

- Build product strategy and requirements from customer discovery, field feedback, ride-alongs, and analytics.
- Own roadmaps and product requirement documents for current and future dock products.
- Partner with hardware, firmware, computer vision, AI, machine learning, design, operations, and GTM teams.
- Drive launch planning, positioning, customer enablement, and reliability improvements.

## What they are asking for

- Experience defining and delivering hardware products with connected software experiences.
- A strong instinct for customer discovery and direct customer learning.
- Interest in reliability, system scaling, data analysis, and hands-on product details.
- Experience with commercial, public safety, or defense hardware markets, or a clear interest in them.

## Why this one is worth a look

Docked drones are where autonomy becomes infrastructure instead of a single device sale. This role sits at that intersection: fleet reliability, physical deployment, user workflows, and the product decisions that make autonomous drone operations repeatable.
