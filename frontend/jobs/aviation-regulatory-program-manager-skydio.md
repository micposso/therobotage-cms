## What the role actually is

Skydio is hiring an aviation regulatory program manager to execute regulatory strategy for advanced autonomous drone operations. The role develops approval requests, waivers, exemptions, declarations, and internal guidance that help customers and partners operate safely and at scale.

This sits at the intersection of product, deployment, regulation, and customer enablement for autonomous drones.

## What you would work on

- Develop and defend operational approval requests, waivers, exemptions, and declarations
- Guide customers and partners through advanced regulatory projects
- Build systems to track and manage regulatory project execution
- Translate emerging regulations into internal guidance and action plans
- Support product demonstrations and technical validation events with flight-test and solutions teams

## What they are asking for

- Aviation regulatory, drone operations, program management, or similar deployment experience
- Ability to turn regulations into practical operating and product guidance
- Strong writing and stakeholder-management skills for approval processes
- Comfort working with customers, partners, product management, engineering, and flight-test teams
- Judgment around safe, compliant, scalable autonomous drone operations

## Why this one is worth a look

Robotics products often need permission structures, not just features. This role is interesting because it helps decide where autonomous drone capability can legally and safely become real customer operations.
