## What the role actually is

Apptronik is hiring a senior electrical engineer for the visual side of Apollo's human-robot interface. The work centers on display and LED lighting electronics, from requirements and architecture through prototypes, validation, production release, and support.

This is an engineering role, but it belongs on the board because the posting explicitly ties the hardware to HRI and asks the person to translate industrial design and UX intent into measurable visual-system requirements.

## What you would work on

- Define electronics requirements for Apollo's visual HRI subsystem
- Design, bring up, and test display and LED lighting PCBAs
- Translate industrial design and UX intent into electrical and optical requirements
- Validate luminance, color, uniformity, viewing angle, thermal behavior, and reliability
- Own visual HRI hardware from concept through production release and support
- Coordinate with suppliers, contract manufacturers, and external design partners

## What they are asking for

- Senior-level experience designing display or LED lighting electronics
- Strong mixed-signal PCB design skills and comfort with modern PCB tools
- Experience with LED drivers, RGB LED arrays, brightness control, color control, and thermal management
- Experience integrating display modules and related power, backlight, and interface electronics
- Ability to design and execute electrical and optical validation tests
- Interest in working with industrial design, mechanical design, and user-facing operator interfaces

## Why this one is worth a look

Many HRI jobs are software or research posts; this one is about the physical signals a humanoid gives to people standing near it. For someone who cares about robot legibility, the role is interesting because the display and lighting hardware will constrain what Apollo can communicate before any app or dashboard is involved.
