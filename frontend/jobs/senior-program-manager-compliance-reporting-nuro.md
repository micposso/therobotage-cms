## What the role actually is

Nuro is hiring a senior program manager for compliance reporting inside the Commercialization and Evaluation function within Software Operations. The role turns regulatory, legal, safety, and operational requirements into reliable reporting programs.

This is a product-adjacent operations role for autonomy: it makes sure external obligations are met with accurate data, clear processes, and documentation that can stand up to review.

## What you would work on

- Own regulatory reporting programs from requirements gathering through submission and follow-up
- Partner with legal, public trust, safety, data platform, and software operations teams
- Build review processes, quality controls, documentation, and audit trails for reporting work
- Translate technical and operational realities into clear reporting plans and stakeholder updates

## What they are asking for

- Program management experience in a regulated, compliance-heavy, or public-sector-adjacent environment
- Strong writing, editing, and documentation skills under deadline pressure
- Ability to work with legal, regulatory, safety, and software operations stakeholders
- Independent judgment for managing multiple workstreams with external timelines

## Why this one is worth a look

Autonomy companies do not scale on model performance alone. Regulatory reporting is part of the operating system around deployed autonomous vehicles, and this role sits in the machinery that helps Nuro stay credible with regulators, partners, and internal safety teams.
