## What the role actually is

Agility is hiring a director of product quality to lead field quality and failure analysis for its deployed commercial humanoid products. The role owns fleet health, reliability performance, issue containment, root-cause analysis, and the feedback loop from customer sites into product design.

This is a product-quality leadership role for robots already working in warehouses and manufacturing environments, where reliability is part of the customer experience.

## What you would work on

- Establish and report field-quality metrics for shipping products and customer deployments
- Lead failure analysis and corrective action across hardware, firmware, supplier, and field issues
- Connect field service, customer sites, supplier quality, and product design teams
- Build processes that prevent repeated failures and improve fleet reliability
- Grow and lead the team responsible for root-cause analysis and fleet-health response

## What they are asking for

- Senior quality or reliability leadership for complex electromechanical products
- Experience with structured problem-solving methods such as 8D, 5-Why, DMAIC, or CAPA
- Ability to work across field operations, customers, suppliers, and design engineering
- Comfort owning quality metrics for deployed commercial products
- Strong judgment under time-sensitive field and customer escalations

## Why this one is worth a look

Humanoid robots will be judged less by demos than by whether fleets stay healthy in ordinary work. This role is worth tracking because it owns the operational evidence that Agility's product is reliable enough for real customer environments.
