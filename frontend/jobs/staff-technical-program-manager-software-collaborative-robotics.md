## What the role actually is

Collaborative Robotics is hiring a staff-level technical program manager to run software programs for Proxie, its collaborative mobile robot. The role covers autonomy, localization, perception, navigation, robot behaviors, and fleet-management systems.

This is a product-shaped TPM role: the person has to turn customer and deployment needs into executable software plans while keeping engineering, hardware, operations, and leadership aligned.

## What you would work on

- Lead planning and delivery for robot software and fleet-management programs
- Coordinate autonomy, behavior, hardware, operations, and customer-facing dependencies
- Translate customer and product needs into technical scope, schedules, and tradeoffs
- Identify risks during development and deployment, then drive cross-functional resolution

## What they are asking for

- Staff-level technical program management experience with complex software or robotics systems
- Ability to work across engineering, product, hardware, operations, and company leadership
- Strong technical judgment around autonomy, deployed systems, or AI-enabled products
- Comfort turning ambiguity into plans that teams can actually execute

## Why this one is worth a look

Collaborative Robotics is trying to make robots that fit into human environments instead of forcing the environment to adapt around them. That makes program leadership unusually important: the software roadmap has to connect robot capability, customer workflows, and reliable behavior around people.
