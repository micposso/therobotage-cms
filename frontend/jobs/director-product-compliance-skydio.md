## What the role actually is

Skydio is hiring a director of product compliance to own compliance across its product portfolio. The role builds the policy, risk framework, requirements inventory, and certification gates that make compliance part of product development from concept through launch.

This is a product leadership role with real launch-readiness authority, not a narrow checklist function.

## What you would work on

- Own product compliance policy, launch-readiness recommendations, and sign-off
- Lead compliance engineers embedded across product programs
- Translate unclear or changing requirements into actionable compliance strategy
- Partner with product and engineering on launch timelines and certification dependencies
- Coordinate expert input across hardware ops, engineering, product, security, supply chain, sales, government, aviation regulatory, and legal

## What they are asking for

- Senior product compliance, certification, regulatory, or safety leadership experience
- Ability to make defensible decisions when requirements are ambiguous or contested
- Experience building compliance systems across multiple product programs
- Strong cross-functional influence with engineering, product, legal, and commercial teams
- Judgment to balance launch timing, certification risk, and customer commitments

## Why this one is worth a look

Compliance is becoming a product capability for drones, especially as deployments expand into government and public-safety use cases. This role matters because it owns the decision layer between product ambition and what can actually launch.
