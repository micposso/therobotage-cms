## What the role actually is

Shield AI is hiring a perception engineer to move promising AI research into real autonomous systems. The role evaluates multimodal computer vision, visual foundation models, and embodied AI methods, then turns the useful pieces into Hivemind perception capabilities.

This is a research-to-product role: experiments and prototypes matter, but so do sensor constraints, edge deployment, testing, documentation, and adoption by autonomy teams.

## What you would work on

- Evaluate emerging perception and embodied-AI methods against representative autonomy data
- Build prototypes that expose where models work, fail, and can be deployed
- Combine learned and classical approaches for detection, tracking, geometric vision, or sensor fusion
- Optimize and integrate perception capabilities for edge robotics systems
- Carry successful experiments into tested Hivemind capabilities that other teams can adopt

## What they are asking for

- Research depth in computer vision, robotics, machine learning, or embodied learning
- Experience with multimodal vision, visual foundation models, video understanding, or open-vocabulary perception
- Strong Python skills and practical work with PyTorch or a similar machine learning framework
- Ability to design careful experiments, understand model failures, and build substantial research software
- Interest in production-quality C++ and collaboration with product engineering teams

## Why this one is worth a look

This is one of the cleaner bridges between the current foundation-model wave and deployed robotics. The caveat is that it sits inside defense autonomy, but the technical problem is exactly the one many robot companies are wrestling with: making general AI perception useful on real hardware under real constraints.
