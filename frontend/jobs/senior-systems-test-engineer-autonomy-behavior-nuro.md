## What the role actually is

Nuro is hiring a senior systems test engineer focused on autonomy behavior. The role standardizes and automates verification and validation processes, builds triage workflows, and improves testing pipelines that generate evidence for autonomy development and the safety case.

This is a technical evaluation role at the checkpoint between autonomy behavior and deployment readiness.

## What you would work on

- Build a unified automated architecture for autonomy validation
- Design and manage triage workflows for validation results
- Improve test pipelines that guide data-driven autonomy development
- Generate test evidence that supports Nuro's autonomy safety case
- Work across systems engineering, autonomy, hardware, software, and robot operations

## What they are asking for

- Experience with systems testing, validation, verification, or autonomy behavior evaluation
- Ability to automate and standardize complex technical test workflows
- Strong systems thinking across hardware, software, and robot operations
- Comfort creating metrics, evaluation methods, and criteria for success
- Bias toward turning test results into actionable deployment evidence

## Why this one is worth a look

The important question in autonomy is often not whether a model works once, but whether the organization can repeatedly prove it works well enough. This role is a concrete example of that discipline becoming a dedicated job.
