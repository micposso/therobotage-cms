## What the role actually is

Nuro is hiring a senior program manager for perception data operations in Mountain View. The team manages training and evaluation data across sensor modalities, translating perception roadmap needs into collection, labeling, vendor, and delivery plans.

This is not a classic product manager role, but it sits in a product-like coordination layer for autonomy: deciding what data is needed, when it is good enough, and how it supports model progress.

## What you would work on

- Turn perception roadmap requirements into data collection and labeling plans
- Coordinate internal teams and external labeling partners against autonomy milestones
- Track quality, throughput, budget, and delivery risks for perception data programs
- Help perception teams get the evidence they need to improve and evaluate models

## What they are asking for

- Program management experience with ML, data operations, autonomy, or complex technical systems
- Ability to translate ambiguous model needs into concrete execution plans
- Comfort managing vendors, timelines, quality metrics, and cross-functional dependencies
- Interest in the practical data loop behind L4 autonomy

## Why this one is worth a look

Autonomy improves through data loops, not only better models. This role is a useful behind-the-scenes view of how perception teams turn messy real-world sensor data into the structured work that makes a robot driver better.
