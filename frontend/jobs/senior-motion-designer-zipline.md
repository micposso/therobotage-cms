## What the role actually is

Zipline is hiring a senior motion designer for its communications and brand work. The role turns product launches, technical concepts, operational footage, interfaces, maps, diagrams, and customer stories into finished motion assets.

This is a hands-on creative role for someone who can make autonomous logistics clear and credible without flattening the technical details.

## What you would work on

- Design and animate motion graphics for product launches, explainers, social video, events, web, and presentations
- Translate briefs, scripts, brand systems, and technical information into storyboards, style frames, animatics, and final animation
- Build visual explanations of Zipline products and operations using typography, maps, diagrams, data, interfaces, callouts, and 2D or 3D animation
- Composite graphics into live-action footage, including screen replacements, tracked graphics, masks, cleanup, and shot-based effects
- Collaborate with communications, brand, social, marketing, engineering, operators, video production, and editing partners
- Maintain reusable templates, animation components, title systems, project files, and production documentation

## What they are asking for

- Professional motion design experience with a strong reel or portfolio
- Expert After Effects ability, plus strong working proficiency in Illustrator, Photoshop, and Figma
- 3D animation experience in Cinema 4D, Blender, or a similar package
- Strong typography, composition, color, hierarchy, timing, pacing, and animation fundamentals
- Ability to explain complex products, systems, data, and technical concepts through motion
- Experience with robotics, autonomy, aviation, logistics, hardware, infrastructure, healthcare, or another technical field is a plus

## Why this one is worth a look

This is not a core robot-building job, but it belongs on the board because Zipline's U.S. expansion depends on making autonomous delivery understandable and acceptable to customers, communities, and partners. The role is a useful signal that product storytelling around robots is becoming more technical, more operational, and more central to deployment.
