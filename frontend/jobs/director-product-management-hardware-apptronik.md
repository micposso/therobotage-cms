## What the role actually is

Apptronik is hiring a senior product leader to own hardware product management across Apollo, related peripherals, and core components. The role sits between robotics engineering, manufacturing, commercialization, and customer-facing teams.

This is not a narrow feature PM role. It is product ownership for the physical platform itself: deciding what hardware capabilities matter, how they sequence, and how they support the humanoid's path from pilots into scaled deployments.

## What you would work on

- Define hardware product vision and roadmap for Apollo and related systems
- Translate customer outcomes, manufacturing constraints, and field needs into product priorities
- Partner with engineering and manufacturing leaders on build, release, and scaling decisions
- Build the product management function around Apptronik's hardware portfolio

## What they are asking for

- Product leadership experience with complex hardware, robotics, or hardware/software systems
- Ability to turn technical constraints and market signals into a coherent roadmap
- Comfort working across engineering, operations, manufacturing, sales, and customers
- Experience leading or building product teams in a high-ambiguity environment

## Why this one is worth a look

Humanoid robotics needs product leadership that treats the machine as a deployable product, not just a research platform. This role is interesting because it owns the hardware decisions that shape what Apollo can realistically do for customers in the field.
