## What the role actually is

Kodiak is hiring a senior product manager to own part of its Level 4 autonomous trucking product across customer applications. The role sits between autonomy, operations, business needs, and field deployment.

This is a product leadership role for a technical robotics system that has to work in real freight and defense contexts. The person is expected to manage the lifecycle of a product area, define priorities, and turn customer and operational feedback into roadmap decisions.

## What you would work on

- Own the lifecycle and roadmap for a Level 4 autonomous trucking product area
- Translate customer, operations, and deployment needs into product requirements
- Partner with engineering, operations, safety, business, and customer-facing teams
- Use operational data and field feedback to prioritize product improvements
- Support autonomous-truck deployments across commercial and public-sector use cases

## What they are asking for

- Product management experience with technical software, hardware, or autonomy products
- Ability to work across engineering, operations, commercial, and customer teams
- Strong judgment around product tradeoffs, data, deployment readiness, and safety
- Comfort owning ambiguous product areas in a complex robotics environment
- Technical background or equivalent experience with autonomous systems or adjacent domains

## Why this one is worth a look

Kodiak is one of the more serious autonomous-trucking companies still pushing toward deployed freight operations. This role is interesting because it is not a pure strategy job: the product decisions have to survive real roads, customer constraints, safety cases, and operations teams that need the system to be usable at scale.
