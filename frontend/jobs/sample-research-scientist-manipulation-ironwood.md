## What the role actually is

Sample listing for local development. A research role attached to a product team rather than isolated from one. You choose problems, publish, and are accountable for at least some of the work reaching a robot a customer uses.

## What you would work on

- Set the research direction for learned manipulation policies over a multi-year horizon
- Publish at the major venues, with the compute and the hardware access to compete
- Work with the engineering team on the unglamorous question of what survives contact with a real gripper

## What they are asking for

- Strong publication record in robot learning or manipulation
- Has taken at least one research result to something that ran on real hardware repeatedly
- Willing to work on problems chosen partly for their product relevance

## Why this one is worth a look

The publish-and-ship combination is genuinely uncommon, and it cuts both ways. You get hardware access and real problems. You also get roadmap pressure that a pure lab would not put on you. Worth asking directly how that tension has been resolved before.
