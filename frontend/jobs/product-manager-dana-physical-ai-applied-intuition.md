## What the role actually is

Applied Intuition is hiring a product manager for Dana, its platform for building agents, apps, and data workflows for physical AI. The role owns shared platform capabilities while also driving hands-on product execution for one or more autonomy verticals.

This is a technical product role for someone who can work with autonomy customers, internal engineering teams, design, TPMs, and go-to-market partners.

## What you would work on

- Define what Dana should build from customer use cases, market needs, and business goals
- Decide which capabilities should be platform-wide versus tailored to specific autonomy verticals
- Partner with engineering and design on product direction, tradeoffs, prioritization, and release work
- Present capabilities to customers, gather feedback, and turn field learning into roadmap decisions
- Support positioning, launch material, pricing, and customer engagement with sales and marketing

## What they are asking for

- Product management experience in a technical company or high-growth startup
- Ability to build trust across engineering and learn complex autonomy workflows quickly
- Technical fluency with software products, platform decisions, and customer-facing tradeoffs
- Comfort owning product direction in a high-visibility, fast-moving environment

## Why this one is worth a look

Dana sits in the tooling layer around physical AI rather than a single robot program. That makes the role interesting for product people who want leverage across cars, trucks, mining, and other autonomy domains, while still staying close to real customer workflows.
