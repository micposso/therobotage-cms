## What the role actually is

Bear Robotics is hiring a product manager in Redwood City for its hospitality robot products. The role owns product strategy, requirements, roadmap priorities, launch planning, and post-launch feedback loops across engineering, design, marketing, sales, and customer teams.

This is a field-aware product job: the person is expected to visit customer sites, observe real robot usage, and turn that feedback into clearer product decisions.

## What you would work on

- Build and refine product roadmaps for Bear's service robot portfolio
- Translate user research, customer-site observations, and usage data into product requirements
- Prioritize features by business value, user impact, and technical feasibility
- Coordinate launches and adoption work with engineering, design, marketing, sales, and customer teams

## What they are asking for

- Product management experience with robotics, hardware systems, or similarly technical products
- Ability to gather user requirements and translate them into clear specifications
- Comfort working in dynamic customer environments and visiting customer sites
- Strong analytical, communication, and cross-functional product execution skills

## Why this one is worth a look

Hospitality robots live or die by whether they fit smoothly into noisy, people-heavy service environments. This role is interesting because it puts customer observation and user-centered product work directly into the roadmap loop, rather than treating deployment feedback as a support-only concern.
