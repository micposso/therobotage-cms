## What the role actually is

Sample listing for local development. You own firmware on custom actuator boards: current control, safety limits, and the communication layer that carries it all to the host. The actuators are compliant by design, so the firmware and the mechanism cannot be developed independently.

## What you would work on

- Close the current and torque loops on new actuator hardware as it comes off the bench
- Build the fault detection that stops a joint before it damages itself or a person
- Own the CAN protocol between joints and the host controller

## What they are asking for

- Has shipped motor control firmware on hardware you helped bring up
- Reads schematics and can hold your own in a design review with the electrical team
- Treats an oscilloscope as a normal debugging tool

## Why this one is worth a look

Small team, custom silicon-adjacent hardware, and unusually short distance between a firmware change and a mechanical consequence. If you like the actuator layer specifically, there are not many teams doing this work at this depth. No salary band is published, which is worth raising early.
