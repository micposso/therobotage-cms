## What the role actually is

Sample listing for local development. You own enterprise accounts in energy, running deals that involve operations, safety, and procurement, each of which can stop the deal for different reasons. Cycles are long and the pilot is usually the real negotiation.

## What you would work on

- Run full-cycle enterprise deals from first conversation through signed pilot and expansion
- Navigate the safety review, which is frequently the actual gate rather than price
- Bring field failure patterns back to product with enough specificity to be useful

## What they are asking for

- Has closed enterprise deals with a hardware or capital-equipment component
- Comfortable at an industrial site, not only in a conference room
- Can hold a technical conversation without overpromising

## Why this one is worth a look

Field robotics in energy is one of the few robotics segments with unambiguous existing budget, which makes the sale easier than most of this industry. No base salary band is published, which for a commission-heavy role is worth pinning down in the first conversation.
