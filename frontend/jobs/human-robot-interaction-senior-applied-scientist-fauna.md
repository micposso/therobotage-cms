## What the role actually is

Fauna is looking for a senior applied scientist to advance human-robot interaction for domestic robots. The role appears focused on interaction models that make a robot feel personal, expressive, and socially aware without losing reliability.

This is a research role with product consequences. It asks someone to connect technical methods, interaction design, evaluation, and the everyday reality of robots sharing space with people.

## What you would work on

- Lead research into social, conversational, and expressive robot behavior
- Design interaction approaches that use context, memory, and multimodal cues
- Evaluate whether robot behavior is legible, trustworthy, and appropriate
- Collaborate across robotics engineering, product design, and user research

## What they are asking for

- Senior applied science background in HRI, AI interaction, robotics, or related fields
- Experience translating ambiguous interaction goals into measurable research programs
- Ability to work across model behavior, human factors, and embodied constraints
- Strong communication with design and engineering partners

## Why this one is worth a look

Senior HRI roles for consumer robots are still rare. This one is interesting because the research question is not only whether the robot can perform a task, but whether its behavior can support trust and repeated use at home.
