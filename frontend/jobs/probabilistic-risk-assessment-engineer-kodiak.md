## What the role actually is

Kodiak is hiring a probabilistic risk assessment engineer for its Autonomy Safety team. The role focuses on estimating and reducing risk in autonomous trucking through Bayesian models, simulations, hazard analysis, fault trees, and validation strategy.

For this board, the relevance is the evaluation layer: the work shapes how a deployed autonomous vehicle company proves that its system is safe enough to operate.

## What you would work on

- Estimate autonomy risk using Bayesian probabilistic models
- Search for failure modes in high-dimensional autonomy scenarios
- Run simulations and analyses that inform engineering priorities
- Perform hazard analysis, fault tree analysis, and structured risk assessment
- Help define validation strategies for autonomous truck safety

## What they are asking for

- Deep systems thinking for autonomy safety and risk assessment
- Strong mathematical grounding in probability, modeling, or related analysis
- Ability to use Python, C++, simulation, and data analysis to reason about rare but severe failures
- Experience connecting safety analysis to engineering decisions and validation plans
- Comfort working on an open-ended safety problem without a simple textbook answer

## Why this one is worth a look

Safety roles can become process-heavy, but this one is framed as a frontier modeling and evaluation problem. Kodiak is asking how to quantify and prove safety for autonomous trucks in the real world, which makes the work unusually central to whether the product can scale.
