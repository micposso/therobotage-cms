## What the role actually is

Boston Dynamics is hiring a principal product manager in Waltham for Atlas logistics robotics. The role defines product strategy for humanoid logistics automation, translating warehouse customer workflows, pain points, and deployment criteria into product roadmaps and requirements.

This is a senior product role at the point where Atlas has to become a usable logistics product, not just an impressive robotics platform.

## What you would work on

- Build business plans and roadmaps for Atlas logistics solutions
- Lead product discovery with logistics customers, prospects, and strategic partners
- Turn warehouse workflows, market gaps, and customer needs into product artifacts and roadmap tradeoffs
- Partner with hardware, software, AI, and go-to-market teams on humanoid logistics applications
- Own product lifecycle work from customer engagement and business cases through prioritization, development, and launch

## What they are asking for

- Senior product management experience or equivalent industry experience
- Ability to create long-term product vision while keeping an executable roadmap for engineering teams
- Experience translating customer processes into requirements for end-to-end robotics solutions
- Comfort working with software and hardware engineering teams in ambiguous environments
- Logistics, warehousing, fulfillment, robotics, or new technology introduction experience

## Why this one is worth a look

Atlas product management is a high-signal role because the core question is practical: what does a humanoid have to do for large logistics customers before it becomes a product they can buy and deploy? The caveat is the seniority and travel: Boston Dynamics expects a customer-facing product leader who can work from warehouse-floor details up to executive strategy.
