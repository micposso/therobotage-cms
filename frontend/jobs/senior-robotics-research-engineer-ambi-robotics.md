## What the role actually is

Ambi Robotics is hiring a senior robotics research engineer in Berkeley. The role focuses on new technology for Ambi's industrial robot products, including AI-driven robot skills, core robotics libraries, perception, motion planning, and evaluation.

The posting is hands-on research engineering tied to production robotic systems. It is especially relevant because Ambi's systems already operate in logistics environments where robot behavior has to be useful, measurable, and deployable.

## What you would work on

- Develop new robot applications and capabilities across controls, planning, and perception
- Lead R&D projects for AI-driven robot skills powered by foundation models
- Contribute to core robotics libraries, training systems, algorithms, and evaluation tooling
- Support deployment of new capabilities to production warehouse robots

## What they are asking for

- Robotics software depth across planning, perception, machine learning, or controls
- Strong C++, Python, Linux, Git, and Docker fluency
- Experience with commercial robotics systems, motion planning, or computer vision
- Ability to lead research projects while staying close to product deployment

## Why this one is worth a look

Warehouse automation is a practical testbed for robot learning: tasks are repetitive, but the real world still refuses to be perfectly tidy. Ambi's role is compelling because it asks research to survive contact with deployed parcel-handling systems.
