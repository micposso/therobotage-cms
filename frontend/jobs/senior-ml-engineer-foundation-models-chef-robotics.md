## What the role actually is

Chef Robotics is hiring a senior ML engineer to work on foundation models for robotic food assembly. The role is centered on model architecture, training objectives, deployment tradeoffs, and learning approaches for deformable-food manipulation.

The listing calls out vision-language-action models, world models, generative approaches, simulation, and real production robot data from Chef's deployed systems.

## What you would work on

- Define architecture and training strategy for Chef's food foundation model
- Evaluate model families including VLAs, world models, and generative approaches
- Use production robot data to improve manipulation policies
- Balance model generalization, sample efficiency, and deployment constraints

## What they are asking for

- Strong ML, robotics, or computer science background through graduate work or equivalent experience
- Experience implementing and deploying ML models for real-world robotics applications
- Hands-on experience with large-scale model training and modern robot-learning methods
- Interest in manipulation problems involving messy, deformable real-world objects

## Why this one is worth a look

Food handling is a difficult manipulation domain because the objects vary constantly and production uptime matters. Chef's pitch is interesting because it pairs a narrow commercial wedge with a growing proprietary manipulation dataset.
