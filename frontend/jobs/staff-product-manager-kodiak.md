## What the role actually is

Kodiak is hiring a staff product manager to act as a senior individual contributor for its Level 4 autonomous trucking platform. The role is meant to own a critical product domain and influence product strategy across customer deployment and commercialization.

This is a high-leverage product role rather than a people-management role. The person is expected to set direction, align executives and cross-functional teams, and make product tradeoffs in a technically complex autonomy environment.

## What you would work on

- Own strategy and execution for a major autonomous-trucking product domain
- Align product direction with customer deployments and commercialization goals
- Partner with autonomy, hardware, operations, safety, and business teams
- Turn technical and operational complexity into clear product decisions
- Communicate roadmap tradeoffs to senior leaders, customers, and cross-functional teams

## What they are asking for

- Senior product management experience with technical, operational, or hardware-adjacent products
- Ability to influence executive stakeholders and cross-functional leaders
- Strong product judgment around customer deployment, autonomy, and commercialization
- Clear communication around complex technical and business tradeoffs
- Comfort operating with high ownership in an autonomous-vehicle company

## Why this one is worth a look

This is a useful signal that Kodiak is building a serious product layer around autonomy, not just an engineering program. The role is about deciding what should ship, why it matters to customers, and how a Level 4 trucking system becomes a durable business.
