## What the role actually is

Shield AI is hiring a senior staff TPM for Hivemind Enterprise, the software development kit used to build unmanned systems and mission applications. The role centers on software quality, simulation-based test automation, release management, and flight-test operations.

This is a product execution role for autonomy software: the person would coordinate product managers, designers, customer-facing teams, and engineering groups so complex releases ship with enough evidence behind them.

## What you would work on

- Drive planning and delivery for Hivemind Enterprise software releases
- Build rigorous simulation-based test automation programs for autonomy software
- Coordinate test operations from CI through hardware-in-the-loop and flight test
- Define scope, milestones, risks, owners, and release-readiness criteria
- Work with product, design, customer engagement, and engineering teams on delivery quality

## What they are asking for

- Senior TPM, engineering management, or software test leadership experience
- Background with complex software, integrated hardware/software systems, AI, or robotics
- Ability to run release management and test operations across multiple technical teams
- Strong communication and ownership in high-stakes autonomy programs

## Why this one is worth a look

Hivemind Enterprise is Shield AI's attempt to make autonomy software usable beyond one internal aircraft program. The role is worth watching because simulation, test automation, and release discipline are what decide whether an autonomy platform can actually be adopted by other teams.
