## What the role actually is

Shield AI is hiring a presentation designer in Washington, D.C. to create high-impact decks and reusable presentation systems for complex defense autonomy products. The work sits with marketing and business development, but the day-to-day design problem is information design for technical systems.

The role is about making product, engineering, and mission concepts legible in slides without flattening the details that matter.

## What you would work on

- Design polished presentation decks from rough outlines, sketches, and stakeholder input
- Build slide templates, libraries, product decks, sales decks, and executive materials
- Translate technical diagrams, workflows, data, and system concepts into clear visuals
- Partner with marketing, product marketing, business development, and solutions engineering
- Maintain visual hierarchy, data integrity, and reusable presentation components

## What they are asking for

- Strong information-design judgment for quantitative, technical, and conceptual material
- Experience building PowerPoint templates, slide libraries, and flagship product or sales decks
- Ability to simplify complex systems without distorting the underlying meaning
- Skill with charts, process flows, system diagrams, timelines, and presentation graphics
- Familiarity with tools such as Figma, Illustrator, or Photoshop

## Why this one is worth a look

This is not a classic robotics UX role, but it belongs on the board because technical storytelling is part of how autonomous systems get understood, bought, and deployed. It is a narrower design role than product design, with more focus on executive and customer communication than interface ownership.
