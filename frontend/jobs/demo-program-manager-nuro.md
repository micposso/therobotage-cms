## What the role actually is

Nuro is hiring a demo program manager to own recurring and bespoke autonomous vehicle demonstrations. The job covers planning, dry runs, technical escalation, day-of coordination, and post-demo follow-through.

The role is not just event logistics. It requires translating demo requests into routes, vehicle readiness, software requirements, operator staffing, contingency plans, and go/no-go decisions.

## What you would work on

- Own the source-of-truth demo calendar, intake flow, requirements, timelines, and stakeholder updates
- Coordinate vehicles, routes, software releases, operator staffing, dry runs, and day-of execution
- Triage issues across mapping, routing, autonomy, controls, fleet, connectivity, and operations tooling
- Track risks, decisions, logs, run IDs, operator feedback, and follow-up actions after each demo

## What they are asking for

- Strong program management ability in technical or operations-heavy environments
- Comfort coordinating across autonomy, operations, fleet, mapping, and leadership stakeholders
- Ability to handle live technical issues and escalate clearly under time pressure
- Organized communication and documentation habits for high-visibility demonstrations

## Why this one is worth a look

Autonomous vehicle demos are a surprisingly good test of whether a robotics product is real. This role owns the moment where route planning, vehicle readiness, software behavior, and executive or partner expectations all collide.
