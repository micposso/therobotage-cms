## What the role actually is

Shield AI is hiring a growth marketing manager to own web experience and content journeys across its digital surfaces. The role supports audiences such as customers, partners, policymakers, recruits, media, and industry stakeholders.

This is a UX/content strategy role inside a defense autonomy company. The person would partner with product marketing, communications, creative, recruiting, business teams, and an agency to make complex products easier to understand online.

## What you would work on

- Maintain, improve, and launch website pages and digital experiences
- Translate campaign, product, recruiting, and communications goals into clear web journeys
- Improve navigation, page flow, content hierarchy, conversion paths, and accessibility
- Coordinate agency work across design, development, SEO, analytics, and site maintenance

## What they are asking for

- Experience in web experience, digital experience, content strategy, UX, or web production
- Hands-on CMS publishing experience, especially WordPress or similar tools
- Strong UX judgment for structuring multi-audience digital journeys
- Comfort using AI-enabled design, content, prototyping, or workflow tools

## Why this one is worth a look

Defense autonomy companies have an unusually hard communication problem: the products are technical, regulated, and often mission-critical. This role is worth tracking because it owns the public web layer where those products become understandable, but it is a marketing web role rather than an embedded product design seat.
