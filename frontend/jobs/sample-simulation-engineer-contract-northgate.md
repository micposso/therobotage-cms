## What the role actually is

Sample listing for local development. A scoped contract engagement on sim-to-real fidelity. Contact dynamics in simulation currently disagree with hardware badly enough that teams have stopped trusting simulation results for manipulation.

## What you would work on

- Quantify where simulation and hardware diverge, before changing anything
- Improve contact and friction modeling for the manipulation cases that matter most
- Leave behind a validation suite the internal team can keep running

## What they are asking for

- Deep familiarity with at least one physics engine used for robotics
- Has done sim-to-real transfer work and can describe what did not work
- Comfortable delivering into an existing codebase you did not write

## Why this one is worth a look

A well-scoped contract with a clear deliverable and an unusually concrete definition of done. Six months, remote within the US, hourly. Contract terms mean no equity and no benefits, which the rate should be read against.
