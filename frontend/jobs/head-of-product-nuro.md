## What the role actually is

Nuro is hiring a Head of Product to own company-level product strategy for its autonomous-driving platform. The role turns customer needs, partner requirements, technical constraints, and commercial priorities into a roadmap for the Nuro Driver and related deployment products.

This is a senior product leadership role at the center of autonomy commercialization. The person would work directly with executive leadership and across autonomy, systems, infrastructure, data, operations, business development, and legal.

## What you would work on

- Define and drive the product roadmap for Nuro's autonomy platform and commercialization products
- Translate customer and partner needs into clear product requirements and technical priorities
- Align autonomy, systems, operations, infrastructure, data, and commercial teams around deployment tradeoffs
- Represent Nuro's technical capabilities externally while turning opportunities into internal product direction

## What they are asking for

- Product leadership experience with complex technical products
- Ability to connect customer needs, operational constraints, and technical feasibility
- Strong cross-functional judgment across engineering, operations, partnerships, and commercialization
- Comfort working close to executive leadership on strategy, roadmap, and market-facing product decisions

## Why this one is worth a look

Nuro is moving from building autonomous-driving technology toward scaling it through vehicle and mobility partners. This role is worth tracking because it sits where autonomy becomes a product business: deciding what gets built, which use cases matter first, and how technical capability becomes deployable value.
