## What the role actually is

Agility is looking for a senior product marketing manager to translate its humanoid robotics portfolio into market language that industrial buyers can understand. The work covers Digit, Arc automation software, customer personas, sales enablement, product launches, and strategic messaging.

This is a product strategy role rather than an engineering role. The person is expected to understand the technology deeply enough to explain it accurately, but the output is positioning, go-to-market clarity, and buyer education.

## What you would work on

- Own positioning and messaging for Digit, Arc, and the broader Agility product portfolio
- Lead go-to-market planning, launch narratives, and audience definition
- Build sales enablement assets such as case studies, spec sheets, presentations, and product videos
- Turn analyst research, customer feedback, competitive intelligence, and win/loss data into market strategy

## What they are asking for

- Senior product marketing or product management experience in robotics, AI, or adjacent deep-tech hardware
- Strong storytelling and writing skills for both technical buyers and non-technical executives
- Familiarity with manufacturing, logistics, or warehousing buyers
- Ability to collaborate with Product Management, Sales, Engineering, and creative teams without owning all of those functions

## Why this one is worth a look

Humanoid robotics has a translation problem: the buyer needs confidence in outcomes, safety, support, integration, and ROI, not just a demo video. This role sits exactly at that boundary. It is a good fit for someone who can make a complex robotics product legible to industrial customers without flattening the technical reality.
