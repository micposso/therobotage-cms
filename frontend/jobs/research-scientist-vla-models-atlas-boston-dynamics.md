## What the role actually is

Boston Dynamics is hiring a research scientist for VLA models on Atlas. The role focuses on large behavior models that use vision, language, task context, and robot state to produce grounded actions for humanoid manipulation.

This is robot-learning research with a direct hardware loop, not a purely offline modeling job.

## What you would work on

- Architect and train VLA and large behavior models for mobile manipulation
- Build imitation learning pipelines from demonstrations, teleoperation, and simulation data
- Develop policies that generalize across manipulation tasks and environments
- Create hierarchical behavior systems for longer-horizon Atlas tasks
- Integrate learned components into the Atlas autonomy stack and test them on hardware

## What they are asking for

- Graduate-level machine learning, robotics, computer science, or related research background
- Experience training and deploying learned policies for robots or simulated characters
- Depth in large behavior models, sequence modeling, or multimodal learning
- Practical experience with PyTorch, JAX, and large-scale training workflows
- Comfort debugging learned policies on physical robotic systems

## Why this one is worth a look

Atlas roles are a useful signal for where humanoid robotics is moving: away from demos as isolated behaviors and toward reusable learned skills. The caveat is that this is a deep technical research seat, but its output will shape how people eventually interact with a general-purpose humanoid.
