## What the role actually is

Fauna is hiring an applied scientist focused on robot navigation in Sunnyvale. The role spans classical navigation, learning-based methods, foundation models, simulation, on-device evaluation, and sim-to-real transfer.

The human-facing angle is practical: navigation failures are user experience failures when robots share indoor spaces with people. This role is about making robot movement more reliable, measurable, and safe in messy environments.

## What you would work on

- Develop navigation methods for indoor environments with static and moving obstacles
- Build simulation and on-device evaluation benchmarks for navigation behavior
- Run sim-to-real transfer experiments and close reliability gaps
- Compare classical, learning-based, and foundation-model approaches for autonomy

## What they are asking for

- Applied science experience in machine learning, robotics, navigation, or related fields
- Strong programming ability in Python, C++, Java, or similar languages
- Experience publishing, patenting, or evaluating technical research
- Comfort turning experimental methods into product-relevant robot behavior

## Why this one is worth a look

Navigation is where autonomy becomes visible to users. A home robot can have a clever model and still feel broken if it hesitates, blocks paths, or moves unpredictably, so this is a research role with a direct line to lived product experience.
