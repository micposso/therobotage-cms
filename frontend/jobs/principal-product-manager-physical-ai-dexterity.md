## What the role actually is

Dexterity is hiring a principal product manager to lead application strategy for retail and third-party logistics customers using its Mech platform. The role owns product lines, developer interfaces, roadmaps, requirements, customer discovery, and the path from one-off deployment learning into repeatable products.

This is a product role close to deployed robotics. The person has to understand warehouse workflows well enough to turn customer-floor evidence into product direction, commercial targets, and technical priorities.

## What you would work on

- Define product strategy and roadmaps for retail and 3PL robotics applications
- Shape developer interfaces that let partners build new applications and integrations
- Translate deployment evidence into PRDs, specifications, and market-entry decisions
- Lead product validation on customer floors through KPI proof and acceptance milestones

## What they are asking for

- Principal-level product management experience with complex technical products
- Ability to work across sales, marketing, customer success, and engineering
- Comfort using customer discovery and market segmentation to choose product priorities
- Willingness to travel for customer and deployment work

## Why this one is worth a look

Dexterity is trying to make physical AI feel like a repeatable product category rather than custom warehouse automation each time. The interesting product question is how much of the messy customer workflow can become a reusable application layer without losing the realities of the floor.
