## What the role actually is

Nuro is hiring a senior or staff research scientist for generative modeling in planning. The role targets the research layer behind autonomy decisions: modeling possible futures, improving planning quality, and validating learned methods for real vehicles.

The seniority suggests this person would influence research direction as well as implementation. It is a fit for researchers who want their work to shape deployed physical AI systems.

## What you would work on

- Set research direction for generative modeling approaches to planning
- Build and evaluate learned planners for autonomous driving use cases
- Connect model behavior to safety, simulation, and production-readiness constraints
- Collaborate across autonomy, infrastructure, and evaluation teams

## What they are asking for

- Senior research experience in ML, autonomy, robotics, or planning
- Depth in generative modeling or learning-based decision-making
- Ability to lead ambiguous research work toward practical outcomes
- Strong judgment about evaluating models in safety-critical physical systems

## Why this one is worth a look

This is the more senior version of a theme showing up across autonomy teams: learned planning is becoming central, but the hard part is proving that it behaves well enough outside the benchmark.
