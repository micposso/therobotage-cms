## What the role actually is

Applied Intuition is hiring a technical program manager for global autonomous trucking programs. The role owns milestones for OEM customer engagements and helps launch autonomous trucking operations across sites, lanes, and regions.

This is a delivery role for L4 autonomy programs, with engineering, operations, safety, facilities, and customer teams all tied into the plan.

## What you would work on

- Own integrated schedules and milestones for autonomous trucking engagements
- Lead operational launches for new sites, routes, and regions
- Coordinate across engineering, fleet, safety, facilities, and customer teams
- Make risks, blockers, and tradeoffs visible early enough to act on them

## What they are asking for

- Technical program management experience in autonomy, trucking, automotive, robotics, or adjacent complex systems
- Ability to work daily with engineering and customer organizations
- Strong execution discipline around launch planning, dependencies, and status
- Comfort working onsite in Sunnyvale

## Why this one is worth a look

Autonomous trucking programs are unforgiving because the product, field operation, and safety case have to mature together. This role is close to that pressure point, making it more interesting than a generic TPM listing.
