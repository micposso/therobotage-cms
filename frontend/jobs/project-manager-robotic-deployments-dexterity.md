## What the role actually is

Dexterity is hiring a project manager for robotic deployments. The role owns the practical mechanics of getting warehouse automation systems into customer sites: planning, site logistics, bill of materials coordination, commissioning, training, change management, and deployment documentation.

The interesting part is that the job also asks the project manager to capture deployment lessons and feed them back into process and design improvements.

## What you would work on

- Manage robotic deployments from planning through installation, commissioning, and training
- Coordinate technicians, deployment engineers, design engineers, supply chain teams, and customer stakeholders
- Track site-specific requirements, component sizing, logistics, upgrades, and punch-list work
- Build repeatable templates and project management practices that help field teams deploy faster

## What they are asking for

- Experience managing complex software and hardware deployment projects
- Ability to work with technical and non-technical teams in customer environments
- Familiarity with automation, material handling, logistics, or distribution operations
- Comfort traveling to warehouse and distribution-center sites

## Why this one is worth a look

Field deployment is where robotics companies learn whether their product is truly repeatable. A role like this is close to the customer, close to the robot, and close to the operating model. For someone who wants to work on robot adoption without being a core robotics engineer, this is a direct path into the real scaling problem.
