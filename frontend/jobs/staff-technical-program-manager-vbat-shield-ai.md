## What the role actually is

Shield AI is hiring a staff technical program manager for V-BAT work in Dallas. The role owns integrated program execution across UAS, autonomy, mission systems, customer delivery, test readiness, and cross-functional engineering plans.

This is not a pure operations role. It sits close to the productized aircraft capability: requirements, interfaces, verification, technical baselines, customer acceptance, and delivery cadence all have to line up.

## What you would work on

- Lead execution for complex UAS, autonomy, mission-system, and customer-delivery programs
- Own integrated plans, milestones, critical paths, dependencies, risks, decisions, and change control
- Coordinate system integration and test across hardware, software, autonomy, systems engineering, manufacturing, flight operations, cybersecurity, and field teams
- Build program metrics and dashboards for integration readiness, release performance, risk posture, and requirements verification

## What they are asking for

- Senior technical program, systems engineering, aerospace, defense, robotics, autonomy, or UAS experience
- Experience leading cross-functional programs that deliver integrated products or customer capabilities
- Systems-engineering fluency around requirements, interfaces, verification, validation, test planning, and technical baselines
- Ability to communicate technical status, tradeoffs, risks, and decisions to engineering leaders, executives, customers, and external stakeholders
- Ability to obtain and maintain a DoD security clearance

## Why this one is worth a look

V-BAT is a fielded autonomous aircraft platform, so the program work has real hardware, software, test, and customer constraints. For someone who likes product-adjacent robotics work but wants to stay close to integration and delivery, this is a high-signal TPM role.
