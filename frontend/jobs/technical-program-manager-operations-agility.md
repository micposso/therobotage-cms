## What the role actually is

Agility Robotics is hiring a technical program manager for operations programs tied to Digit deployment. The role bridges supply chain, manufacturing engineering, production, and quality so the operations organization can support the next generation of the humanoid platform.

This is a product-adjacent program role for someone who likes execution systems, risk tracking, and production ramp readiness.

## What you would work on

- Run weekly execution check-ins across supply chain, technical operations, and manufacturing
- Track commitments, risks, and cross-team actions through closure
- Serve as a workstream program manager inside larger efforts such as production ramp readiness and capacity expansion
- Maintain Jira, Confluence, Smartsheet, Excel, program hubs, dashboards, and decision logs
- Prepare monthly operations program reviews with portfolio health, exceptions, and escalations
- Help functional teams adopt a single program-management framework and source of truth

## What they are asking for

- Technical program management experience in operations, hardware, manufacturing, or robotics environments
- Ability to drive cross-functional execution with limited oversight
- Strong written status, risk management, escalation, and decision follow-through habits
- Comfort working hybrid from Agility's Salem operations center

## Why this one is worth a look

This is not a pure product strategy role, but it is close to the make-or-break question for humanoids: whether a company can turn a robot platform into a repeatable deployed product. It pairs well with Agility's already-listed senior operations TPM role for candidates at a different level.
