## What the role actually is

Sample listing for local development. You commission robots at energy sites, diagnose them when they stop, and get them back to work. Roughly half of what you find will be a problem nobody at headquarters knew existed.

## What you would work on

- Commission new units on site and hand them over to operators who have never used one
- Diagnose failures with the tools in your vehicle, not the ones on an engineering bench
- Write up what actually broke, in enough detail that engineering can reproduce it

## What they are asking for

- Comfortable with hand tools, wiring, and a multimeter
- Willing to travel across Gulf Coast sites most weeks
- Can explain a fault to an operator without condescending to them

## Why this one is worth a look

Field roles are the fastest way to learn what robots actually do wrong, and that knowledge transfers to almost every other job on this board. The honest caveat is the travel: this is a road job, not a desk job with occasional trips.
