## What the role actually is

Zipline is hiring a senior producer to help scale its creative and marketing organization. The role owns the production details behind customer-facing campaigns, launches, events, printed materials, direct mail, vehicle wraps, storefront work, and other brand experiences.

This is a production role for a robotics company where the brand shows up in physical places, not just on screens. The person would turn creative ideas into finished work that supports real market launches and customer adoption.

## What you would work on

- Lead production for campaigns, launches, events, and brand initiatives
- Coordinate creative, marketing, operations, vendors, budgets, and timelines
- Turn concepts into physical and digital assets that reach customers in market
- Build repeatable production processes for a growing autonomous delivery brand

## What they are asking for

- Production experience across campaigns, experiential work, events, retail, print, or physical brand systems
- Strong project management judgment around scope, budget, vendors, and deadlines
- Ability to partner closely with creative directors and marketing teams
- Comfort producing work for a fast-moving company with both digital and physical touchpoints

## Why this one is worth a look

Autonomous delivery needs more than an app and a press release. Zipline has aircraft, launch sites, packaging, partner moments, and local market presence. A senior producer can shape how coherent the service feels when it arrives in the real world.
