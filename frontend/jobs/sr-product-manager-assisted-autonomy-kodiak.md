## What the role actually is

Kodiak is hiring a senior product manager for Assisted Autonomy, the part of its autonomous trucking system where a human can remotely control a truck in low-speed, clearly defined scenarios. The role owns the product direction for that human-in-the-loop layer.

This is an IC product role that blends software, hardware, field operations, customer feedback, safety, and UX/design judgment. The listing is explicit that the work includes highly usable interfaces for teleoperations and non-technical users.

## What you would work on

- Formulate and evolve the Assisted Autonomy roadmap across near-term deployment and long-term scale
- Define requirements with engineering, operations, safety, program, and customer-facing teams
- Improve software interfaces for dispatch, telestation capabilities, and related hardware integrations
- Use operational data and KPIs to prioritize failure modes and safety improvements
- Visit deployment locations to gather customer and field feedback

## What they are asking for

- Product management experience in software and hardware environments
- Experience launching high-tech, end-user-facing products at scale
- Strong UX/design judgment for teleoperation and human-in-the-loop workflows
- Ability to reason clearly about data, KPIs, product tradeoffs, and field deployment
- Prior exposure to assisted autonomy, autonomous vehicles, or robotics

## Why this one is worth a look

Assisted autonomy is where the clean story of self-driving meets operational reality. This role is interesting because it treats human control as a product surface, not an exception path: what the remote operator sees, when they intervene, and how the system earns trust.
