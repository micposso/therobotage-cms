## What the role actually is

Applied Intuition is hiring a designer to maintain and extend its brand system. The work spans web, campaigns, presentations, reports, trade shows, signage, collateral, and the visual language around Applied's physical AI platform.

## What you would work on

- Evolve brand guidelines and keep visual execution consistent across channels.
- Create digital assets for web, social, email, ads, decks, reports, iconography, and infographics.
- Design physical-space materials such as trade show booths, signage, brochures, and event collateral.
- Partner with marketing, product, and engineering stakeholders to translate technical work into clear visuals.

## What they are asking for

- A portfolio with digital and print brand design work.
- Fluency with Adobe Creative Suite and Figma or similar design tools.
- Experience designing for web, marketing, and physical collateral.
- Comfort taking feedback from multiple teams and refining visual systems.

## Why this one is worth a look

Most robotics and autonomy companies struggle to make highly technical systems legible. This role sits in the design and marketing layer where Applied's autonomy, defense, and industrial products have to become understandable to customers, partners, and operators.
