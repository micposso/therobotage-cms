## What the role actually is

GrayMatter Robotics is hiring an AI research intern for foundation-model work in Carson, California. The role sits with a special projects AI team building capabilities for robots used in real manufacturing processes.

The posting is unusually applied for an internship: the research is framed around deployed robotic finishing systems, customer sites, and reliability on real parts.

## What you would work on

- Contribute to foundation-model research for industrial robot behavior
- Work with robotics software, applications, hardware, and deployment teams
- Help move AI experiments from data and simulation toward production robots
- Evaluate model performance on real manufacturing variation

## What they are asking for

- AI, robotics, machine learning, or related research training
- Interest in foundation models for physical systems
- Ability to work through ambiguous applied research problems
- Comfort collaborating across robotics and manufacturing teams

## Why this one is worth a look

Many foundation-model robotics roles are still abstract. GrayMatter's version is tied to surface finishing and factory deployments, which makes it a useful signal for where embodied AI is becoming commercially specific.
