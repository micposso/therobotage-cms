## What the role actually is

Apptronik is hiring a staff video producer to lead writing, producing, directing, and post-production for robotics storytelling. The role turns complex hardware, deep tech, and humanoid robotics work into product launches, digital campaigns, and brand narratives.

This is a senior creative production role with hands-on writing, concepting, shoot direction, editing, photography, and cross-functional collaboration.

## What you would work on

- Write, script, storyboard, and pitch video concepts for launches, campaigns, and brand stories
- Lead video projects from idea through delivery, including schedules, shoots, and production logistics
- Direct talent on set and shape camera composition, lighting, and visual approach
- Edit quick-turn digital cuts and work with outside partners on larger finishing or motion needs
- Capture product, behind-the-scenes, and environmental photography for marketing channels
- Partner with executive, product, and engineering teams to translate robotics details into accessible stories

## What they are asking for

- Senior video production strength in hardware, deep tech, robotics, or similarly complex categories
- Strong writing, creative direction, and end-to-end producing ability
- Hands-on editing or photography skills alongside production leadership
- Comfort working with product and engineering teams on technical launches

## Why this one is worth a look

Humanoid robotics companies have to explain not just what a robot is, but why it is credible and useful. This role sits directly in that translation layer for Apollo as Apptronik moves from technical development toward commercialization.
