## What the role actually is

Applied Intuition is hiring a product designer for developer tools and defense products that support autonomy development, testing, deployment, and command-and-control workflows. The work sits at the intersection of autonomy, hardware, software, and operational environments.

This is a full-stack product design role with research, prototyping, user testing, visual design, and design-system responsibilities.

## What you would work on

- Lead design from concept through launch for complex autonomy software applications
- Translate technical and operational requirements into usable interfaces
- Build high-fidelity mockups and interactive prototypes for cross-functional review
- Conduct user research with commercial and defense personnel to understand real workflows
- Work with product managers, engineers, and defense subject matter experts to refine designs
- Advocate for testing and research methods that prove products meet user needs

## What they are asking for

- Product design or UX design experience on complex software applications
- Ability to handle real-time, interactive, or operationally demanding product surfaces
- Comfort translating technical constraints into clear user experiences
- User research and prototyping strength for workflows where reliability matters

## Why this one is worth a look

The listing is notable because it treats defense autonomy software as a design problem, not only an engineering problem. It is a good match for designers who want dense, high-consequence interfaces and can handle the domain constraints.
