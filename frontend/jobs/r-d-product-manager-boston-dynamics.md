## What the role actually is

Boston Dynamics is hiring an R&D product manager in Waltham for work around data capture, digital twins, and embodied AI workflows. The role sits between customers, designers, engineers, and robotics teams to turn field needs into product direction.

This is a product role for the messy middle between advanced robotics research and usable customer workflows.

## What you would work on

- Talk with customers on-site and remotely to understand how they use Boston Dynamics products
- Scope proof-of-concept and pilot engagements that test product value in real settings
- Prioritize and launch platform features with infrastructure, firmware, and software teams
- Work with designers and engineers on software applications that connect internal and external workflows
- Bring customer feedback back into the roadmap after launch

## What they are asking for

- Product management or founder experience with complex technical products
- Experience with digital twins, laser scanning, virtual reality, or game-design-adjacent spatial products
- Ability to turn customer requests into scoped product problems for engineering teams
- Familiarity with robotics, IoT, or similarly physical technical systems
- Comfort with occasional customer-site travel for testing, interviews, and observation

## Why this one is worth a look

The interesting part is the digital-twin angle. Boston Dynamics already has robots that can collect data in the world; this role is about making the capture, interpretation, and customer workflow around that data coherent enough to become a product.
