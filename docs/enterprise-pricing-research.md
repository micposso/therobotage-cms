The public page emphasizes managed training, content reuse, and onboarding support. A portal alone is available for much less. These rates position a bounded managed service between self-service software and a larger production subscription; they do not establish that we are the cheapest or prove willingness to pay.

Basic retains its build budget for discovery, two modules, footage editing, assessments, portal setup, and launch. Growth lowers the larger entry hurdle while retaining six modules and bilingual delivery. Both retain the existing learner/site allowances and four/ten monthly service hours. Twelve-month terms remain explicit. No invented discount anchor or revenue improvement claim is displayed.

## Delivery assumptions and validation before contracting

- Customer supplies approved procedures, usable footage, and subject-matter review. Reusable templates and bounded revision rounds are necessary at these prices. New filming, bespoke animation, major revisions, and additional modules remain separately scoped.
- Confirm platform licensing, account isolation, external-customer use permissions, and reporting before committing. LearnWorlds' ten client groups are not evidence that its listed plan meets Growth's fifteen-site requirements. A customer site is not automatically an isolated LMS tenant.
- Confirm actual delivery cost. At an illustrative 50% gross-margin target, recurring direct costs must stay below $745/month for Basic and $1,745/month for Growth. Build direct-cost budgets would be $3,750 and $7,500. These are planning ceilings, not measured costs or promised margins; include labor, platform, localization, support, and direct overhead.
- If the scoped workload exceeds these budgets, revise scope or quote Enterprise rather than silently removing included services. Separate safety/privacy specialist review and native app work remain outside standard packages.
- Track proposal conversion, objections, actual build hours, recurring effort, and customer onboarding outcomes during early pilots. Revisit rates using delivery evidence. No customer demand or profitability has been validated by this desk research.
