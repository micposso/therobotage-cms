## Commercial hypothesis

Repeated customer training can consume scarce deployment staff time. If training is a genuine bottleneck, transferring repeatable instruction to a managed Academy may release capacity for additional deployments. Actual revenue impact still depends on demand, hardware supply, commissioning capacity and conversion. Training-hour savings alone are not revenue.

## Highest-priority next steps

1. Produce a short sample Academy module for one robot category, including a lesson, supervisor checklist and practical assessment. Let prospects inspect the deliverable before requesting a pilot.
2. Run a measured pilot. Record internal training hours per site, Academy fees, remaining internal work, employee task proficiency and customer onboarding satisfaction. Seek permission for a named case study. Define measurement windows and comparison cohorts before delivery.
3. Test the commercial model with buyers: vendor-funded customer enablement versus a customer-paid Academy add-on bundled into deployment. Establish delivery costs and margin before making claims about a new revenue stream.
4. Bring qualified visitors through targeted outreach to robotics leaders responsible for deployment, customer success and partnerships. Lead with a specific trigger: entering a new language market, opening more customer sites, or repeatedly retraining new hires. Link to the matching use case. No outreach has been sent.
5. Measure qualified inquiries, attended calls, paid pilot conversion and pilot-to-rollout conversion, segmented by acquisition source. CTA clicks alone are not the success metric. Use the existing analytics stack if events are added; none were introduced in this pass.

## Claims and experiments

Do not publish a 30% revenue, adoption or satisfaction improvement without supporting results. Test a capacity-led headline against a cost-led headline, holding traffic source and offer constant. Treat results as hypotheses until enough qualified conversions exist. Prioritize proof and buyer interviews before increasing paid traffic.
